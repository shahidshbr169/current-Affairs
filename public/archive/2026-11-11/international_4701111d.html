US warns Madagascar over growing military ties with Russia

11 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: The United States has cautioned Madagascar against deepening its military cooperation with Russia, warning that a “Sahel‑model” security arrangement could amplify instability across Africa.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Bilateral, regional and global groupings involving India; International Relations – India’s strategic interests in the Indian Ocean Region and Africa.

Key Data & Agreements: Sahel‑model security deal

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: Russia’s outreach to Madagascar mirrors its broader Africa strategy of securing footholds through security pacts, challenging the US‑EU influence and potentially reshaping the security architecture of the continent.
  • Impact on India: A Russian‑Madagascar security nexus could affect India’s maritime interests in the Indian Ocean, complicate counter‑piracy and anti‑terror operations, and influence India’s diplomatic engagement with African Regional Economic Communities (RECs).
  • Way Forward: India should deepen defence‑diplomacy with Madagascar and the wider Indian Ocean littoral, offer capacity‑building alternatives to Russian assistance, and coordinate with the US, EU and African Union to promote a balanced security framework.

Prelims Pointers

  • US‑Russia strategic competition extending to Africa.
  • Madagascar’s potential adoption of a “Sahel‑model” security pact with Russia.
  • Implications for Indian Ocean security and India’s “Act East” & “Indo‑Pacific” strategies.
  • India’s existing engagement with African RECs (e.g., SADC, AU) as platforms for diplomatic outreach.
