Canada has not made a decision about lifting Arctic drilling ban, says Mark Carney

11 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Canada is reviewing its moratorium on oil and gas exploration in the Arctic, with the finance ministry indicating that a decision on lifting the ban is still pending.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – International Relations: Arctic geopolitics, bilateral & multilateral environmental agreements, and strategic implications for India.

Key Data & Agreements: Arctic drilling ban (Canada) – under review (2024)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The Arctic is emerging as a contested frontier for energy security, new shipping routes (e.g., Northern Sea Route) and climate politics. Canada’s possible policy shift could intensify competition among Arctic states (USA, Russia, Norway, Denmark) and attract interest from non‑Arctic powers such as China.
  • Impact on India:
    • Energy: Potential increase in global oil supply could affect crude price volatility, influencing India’s import bill.
    • Climate: Lifting the ban may undermine global climate mitigation efforts, challenging India’s commitments under the Paris Agreement.
    • Strategic: India, as an observer in the Arctic Council, may need to recalibrate its engagement with Canada and other Arctic stakeholders to safeguard its interests in emerging Arctic shipping lanes and scientific cooperation.
  • Way Forward:
    • Strengthen diplomatic dialogue with Canada to advocate for a precautionary approach that balances energy interests with environmental safeguards.
    • Leverage India’s observer status in the Arctic Council to push for stricter environmental standards and joint research on Arctic climate impacts.
    • Explore renewable energy collaborations with Canada to offset any increase in fossil‑fuel extraction in the region.
