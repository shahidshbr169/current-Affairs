Liberia-flagged VLCC hit in Persian Gulf, six Indian seafarers aboard: Sarbananda Sonowal

11 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: A Liberia‑flagged Very Large Crude Carrier (VLCC) was struck in the Persian Gulf, injuring four crew members, including one Indian seafarer who sustained minor injuries. Six Indian nationals were on board the vessel at the time of the attack.

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The incident underscores the vulnerability of commercial shipping in the Persian Gulf – a critical chokepoint for global oil supplies – amid heightened regional tensions and the rise of non‑state maritime threats.
  • Impact on India: Direct implications include the safety of Indian seafarers, potential disruptions to India’s oil imports, and the need for enhanced naval presence to protect Indian‑flagged and Indian‑crewed vessels transiting the Gulf.
  • Way Forward: India should deepen maritime security cooperation with Gulf states, augment naval patrols under the Indian Ocean Region (IOR) framework, and negotiate crew‑safety protocols with flag states and shipping associations.
