Tamil Nadu to push green cracker production in meeting with Sivakasi manufacturers this month: Minister V.K. Rajeev

11 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The Tamil Nadu government, through Minister V.K. Rajeev, will engage Sivakasi fire‑cracker manufacturers to promote the production of “green” crackers. This initiative follows a spike in air‑pollution levels in Chennai during Deepavali, with Valasaravakkam recording the city’s worst air‑quality readings.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper III – Environment, Ecology & Climate Change; GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (State administration & policy implementation)

Key Data & Stats: Deepavali 2026 Higher pollution levels in Chennai Valasaravakkam – highest AQI in city

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Fire‑cracker usage during Deepavali has long been linked to acute spikes in particulate matter (PM₂.₅, PM₁₀) and nitrogen oxides, prompting citizen complaints and judicial interventions in several states. Sivakasi, a hub for pyrotechnics, is under pressure to adopt cleaner technologies.
  • Significance: Promoting green crackers can curb short‑term air‑quality deterioration, reduce health burdens (respiratory ailments, hospital admissions), and align with national commitments under the National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) and the Paris Agreement.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: • Limited availability of certified green‑cracker technology; • Higher production costs may affect small‑scale manufacturers; • Need for robust monitoring to prevent token compliance; • Potential legal challenges if existing environmental clearances are not updated.
  • Way Forward: • Form a joint task‑force comprising the State Pollution Control Board, Ministry of Environment, Forests & Climate Change, and industry representatives; • Offer fiscal incentives (subsidies, tax rebates) for manufacturers adopting low‑emission formulations; • Conduct awareness drives in high‑pollution zones like Valasaravakkam; • Periodic review by an independent expert committee (e.g., an ARC‑type panel) to assess emission reductions and suggest policy tweaks.

Prelims Pointers

  • Green crackers – fire‑crackers with reduced sulphur and aluminium content.
  • National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) – aims for 20‑30% reduction in PM₂.₅ by 2024‑25.
  • State Pollution Control Board (SPCB) – statutory body for monitoring air quality.
  • Article 21 of the Constitution – right to a healthy environment as part of the right to life.
