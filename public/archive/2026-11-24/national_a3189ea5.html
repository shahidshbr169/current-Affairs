PM Modi speaks to German Chancellor as India-EU free trade pact nears finish line

24 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Prime Minister Narendra Modi and German Chancellor Friedrich Merz held a telephone conversation to review bilateral cooperation, highlighting the accelerating economic and strategic coordination between India and the EU as the India‑EU Free Trade Agreement (FTA) approaches its finalisation.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – International Relations, Economic Governance, Trade Agreements; GS Paper III – Security & Strategic Affairs

Key Data & Stats: India‑EU FTA – near completion (2024) EU is India’s third largest trading partner Bilateral trade grew ≈ 30 % in FY 2023‑24

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Negotiations for a comprehensive India‑EU FTA began in 2007 and have progressed through multiple rounds, culminating in a draft text that covers goods, services, investment, and regulatory cooperation. The call comes amid heightened global supply‑chain realignments and geopolitical tensions, prompting both sides to deepen economic ties.
  • Significance: Finalising the FTA would grant Indian exporters preferential market access to the €27 trillion EU market, potentially boosting sectors such as pharmaceuticals, textiles, and IT services. Strategically, it reinforces India’s “Act East, Connect West” policy and diversifies trade away from traditional partners.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: • Alignment of standards and certifications (e.g., EU’s stringent sanitary‑phytosanitary norms) may delay implementation.
    • Sensitive sectors like agriculture and dairy face domestic resistance.
    • The agreement must be WTO‑compatible; any preferential tariff reductions require notification and may attract disputes.
  • Way Forward: • Set up a joint India‑EU Implementation Committee to resolve technical barriers.
    • Parliament should enact enabling legislation under the Foreign Trade (Development and Regulation) Act, 1992 for smooth tariff adjustments.
    • Engage the ARC or a specialised Trade Facilitation Committee to monitor sector‑wise impact and recommend mitigation for vulnerable domestic producers.

Prelims Pointers

  • India‑EU Free Trade Agreement – first comprehensive FTA with a major economic bloc.
  • EU is India’s third‑largest trading partner; bilateral trade up ≈ 30 % in FY 2023‑24.
  • Strategic coordination under the EU‑India Global Strategic Partnership framework.
  • Modi‑Merz call – diplomatic channel reinforcing economic diplomacy amidst global tensions.
