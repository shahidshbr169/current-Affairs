  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The US‑Canada trade dialogue is a cornerstone of the North American economic architecture (USMCA).   • Impact on India: India’s trade with Canada has surged, especially in pharmaceuticals, education services, and agri‑exports. Changes in US‑Canada trade rules could affect market access for Indian firms operating in North America and alter competitive dynamics for Indian exporters.
  • Way Forward: India should deepen its strategic economic engagement with both the US and Canada—leveraging the US‑Canada dialogue to advocate for transparent, rules‑based trade, and pursuing sector‑specific MoUs (e.g., in clean energy, digital services) to diversify its North‑American market footprint.

  • Canada is among India’s top 10 trading partners, with bilateral trade crossing US$ 15 billion in FY 2023‑24.
  • Both the US and Canada are members of the Quad and G20, offering India platforms for multilateral engagement.
  • India’s diaspora in Canada (~1.5 million) and growing tech‑sector ties make the North‑American market strategically important for India’s services exports.
