Thirupparankundram 'deepathoon' row LIVE: Karthigai Deepam lit at traditional spot despite Supreme Court order

24 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The Arulmigu Subramanian Swamy Temple lit the Karthigai Deepam at the traditional Uchipillayar Deepa Mandapam despite a Supreme Court order prohibiting the practice, prompting police to detain protesting activists outside the Thirupparankundram temple.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (Judicial review, Article 25‑26, role of police); GS Paper III – Social Issues (Religion‑state interface).

Key Data & Stats: Supreme Court order (date not disclosed)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Karthigai Deepam is a major Tamil Hindu festival celebrated by lighting a ‘maha deepam’ in temples. The temple administration invoked “age‑old traditions and Agama rules” to conduct the ritual at the historic Uchipillayar Deepa Mandapam. A prior Supreme Court order—issued in response to concerns over public safety and environmental impact—had directed the temple to refrain from lighting the deepam at that location.
  • Significance: The incident highlights the friction between constitutional guarantees of religious freedom (Art 25‑26) and the judiciary’s power to enforce public‑order considerations. It also tests the limits of state intervention in religious affairs and the operational role of police in upholding court orders.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis:
    • Potential violation of the Supreme Court’s directive, raising questions on contempt of court.
    • Balancing Article 25‑26 rights with Article 19(2) (reasonable restrictions for public order, health, and morality).
    • Law‑enforcement dilemma: ensuring compliance without escalating communal tensions.
    • Absence of a clear statutory framework governing the conduct of religious festivals in heritage sites.
  • Way Forward:
    • Constitute a **Law Commission** committee to draft guidelines on the conduct of large‑scale religious rituals in public or heritage spaces, incorporating safety, environmental, and heritage‑preservation norms.
    • Encourage the **Supreme Court** to issue a detailed judgment clarifying the scope of Article 26 vis‑à‑vis judicial orders.
    • Promote dialogue between temple authorities, local administration, and civil society to identify alternative venues or modified rituals that respect tradition while complying with legal directives.
    • Strengthen police training on handling religious‑sensitive protests, emphasizing minimal force and community‑engagement approaches.

Prelims Pointers

  • Supreme Court order restricting Karthigai Deepam lighting at Uchipillayar Deepa Mandapam.
  • Constitutional provisions: Article 25 (Freedom of Religion) and Article 26 (Management of Religious Affairs).
  • Agama Shastras – traditional Hindu temple codes governing rituals.
  • Role of police in enforcing judicial orders and maintaining public order during religious festivals.
