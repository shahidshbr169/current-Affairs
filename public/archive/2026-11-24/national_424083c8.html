Arrangements in place for Mission Chakkakompan

24 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: The state forest department has finalized operational arrangements for “Mission Chakkakompan” and is also planning a targeted “darting” operation at Murivali in Chinnakkanal to curb human‑wildlife conflict and strengthen forest protection.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper III – Environment, Ecology, Biodiversity & Climate Change; GS Paper II – Governance, Policy & Institutional Frameworks.

Key Data & Stats: N/A

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Over the past few years, increasing incidents of wildlife encroachment and forest degradation in the Chinnakkanal region have prompted the forest department to launch Mission Chakkakompan, a comprehensive forest‑conservation and conflict‑mitigation programme.
  • Significance: Effective implementation will aid in preserving biodiversity, maintaining forest cover, and reducing human‑wildlife clashes, thereby supporting the objectives of the National Forest Policy (1988) and the Forest Conservation Act (1980).
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Operational bottlenecks include limited manpower, need for coordination with local panchayats, potential resistance from communities dependent on forest resources, and ensuring that darting complies with wildlife‑protection norms.
  • Way Forward: • Strengthen Joint Forest Management (JFM) committees for community participation.
    • Periodic monitoring by the State Biodiversity Board.
    • Align the mission with the “Green India Mission” under the National Action Plan on Climate Change.
    • Seek guidance from the ARC on inter‑state wildlife corridors if the area borders neighboring states.

Prelims Pointers

  • Mission Chakkakompan – state‑level forest‑conservation initiative.
  • Forest Conservation Act, 1980 & National Forest Policy, 1988.
  • Article 48A of the Constitution – duty of the State to protect the environment.
  • Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) – overseeing such missions.
