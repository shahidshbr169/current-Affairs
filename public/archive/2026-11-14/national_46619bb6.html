Puri resigns from Uttar Pradesh Rajya Sabha seat after being re-elected from MP

14 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Hardeep Singh Puri, who was a Rajya Sabha member from Uttar Pradesh, resigned from that seat after being elected unopposed to the Madhya Pradesh Rajya Sabha seat on 5 Nov 2024. His new six‑year term will run until 2 Apr 2030, creating a vacancy in the Uttar Pradesh representation.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (Rajya Sabha composition, Article 80, representation of States, anti‑defection provisions).

Key Data & Stats: 5 Nov 2024 – Election date, Unopposed election, Tenure ends 2 Apr 2030, Rajya Sabha strength: 245 members

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Rajya Sabha members are elected by State Legislative Assemblies as per Article 80 of the Constitution. A sitting member may contest from another State, but cannot hold two seats simultaneously; hence resignation is mandatory.
  • Significance: The shift alters the political arithmetic of Uttar Pradesh’s representation in the Upper House and strengthens the ruling party’s numbers from Madhya Pradesh. It also underscores the practice of “strategic resignations” to accommodate senior leaders.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: The vacancy triggers a by‑poll in Uttar Pradesh, incurring additional expenditure and administrative effort. Frequent resignations raise questions on the stability of Rajya Sabha composition and the need for clearer procedural guidelines.
  • Way Forward: A committee (e.g., a Parliamentary Reforms Committee) could recommend a minimum notice period before resignation and a streamlined by‑poll mechanism. Strengthening the anti‑defection law to address mid‑term seat swaps may also be considered.

Prelims Pointers

  • Article 80 – Constitution: composition and election of Rajya Sabha.
  • Rajya Sabha term: 6 years with one‑third retirement every two years.
  • Unopposed election: common in Rajya Sabha when the ruling party has a clear majority in the State Assembly.
  • By‑poll requirement on resignation of a Rajya Sabha member.
