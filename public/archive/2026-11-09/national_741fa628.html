PM Modi to visit Bihar on November 21 as NDA government completes one year in the State

9 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Bihar on 21 Nov 2026 to mark the completion of one year of the NDA‑led state government. The visit coincides with the commencement of the Assembly’s five‑day winter session on 30 Nov 2026, during which a second supplementary budget will be tabled.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; GS Paper III – Economic Development (state finances, budgeting).

Key Data & Stats: 1 year of NDA government in Bihar; 5‑day winter session; Second supplementary budget to be presented; 21 Nov 2026 (PM’s visit); 30 Nov 2026 (session start).

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The NDA coalition, led by the Janata Dal (United) and the Bharatiya Janata Party, formed the state government after the 2025 Bihar Legislative Assembly elections. The first year has seen flagship schemes such as “Bihar Skill Development Mission” and “Kisan Samriddhi Yojana” being rolled out, while fiscal consolidation remains a priority.
  • Significance: The Prime Minister’s visit underscores the Centre‑State synergy under the “Cooperative Federalism” model and provides a platform to showcase developmental achievements before the winter session. The supplementary budget reflects the state’s need to address revenue shortfalls and fund pending welfare programmes, a critical issue for fiscal federalism.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis:
    • Fiscal deficit pressures: reliance on central transfers and borrowing may breach the Article 112 fiscal deficit ceiling.
    • Implementation bottlenecks in flagship schemes due to administrative capacity constraints.
    • Potential political friction in the Assembly, given opposition demands for greater scrutiny of the supplementary budget.
  • Way Forward:
    • Constitution of a high‑powered committee (modeled on the ARC) to audit the supplementary budget’s revenue‑expenditure matching.
    • Strengthening the State Finance Commission’s role to ensure equitable allocation of central funds.
    • Adoption of a phased implementation framework for welfare schemes with real‑time monitoring via the Integrated State Dashboard.
    • Encourage greater participation of civil society and opposition legislators in budget discussions to enhance transparency.

Prelims Pointers

  • Article 174 – Constitution of State Legislatures and their sessions.
  • Article 112 – Fiscal deficit limits for states.
  • State Finance Commission – constitutional body for Centre‑State financial relations.
  • Supplementary Budget – an additional financial statement presented after the main budget to meet unforeseen expenditures.
