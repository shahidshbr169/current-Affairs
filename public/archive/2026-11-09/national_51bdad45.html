Andhra Pradesh ranks second in Access Equality Index, but 13th in per capita income

9 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Andhra Pradesh secured the 2nd position in the Access Equality Index, reflecting strong performance in basic infrastructure, health, education, socio‑economic security and access to justice. However, the state lags in income generation, ranking 13th in per‑capita income, highlighting a gap between service delivery and economic prosperity.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Social Justice & Welfare; GS Paper I – Indian Economy (inclusive growth, regional disparities)

Key Data & Stats: 2nd rank – Access Equality Index, 13th rank – Per‑capita income, 5 assessment areas: basic infrastructure, healthcare, education, socio‑economic security, access to justice

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The Access Equality Index, compiled by a national think‑tank (e.g., NITI Aayog), evaluates states on five pillars of public service delivery to gauge equitable access across populations.
  • Significance: A high rank signals effective implementation of welfare schemes and administrative capacity, aligning with constitutional commitments under Articles 41 & 46 for socio‑economic welfare. The income ranking, however, points to structural economic challenges that can undermine long‑term inclusive growth.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Persistent low per‑capita income may stem from limited industrial base, inadequate skill development, and uneven rural‑urban growth. The disparity raises concerns about fiscal sustainability of welfare programmes and the risk of “service‑rich but income‑poor” development models.
  • Way Forward: • Strengthen livelihood and skill‑training initiatives (e.g., Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana).
    • Promote sector‑specific investment through state‑level industrial policy and public‑private partnerships.
    • Institutionalise periodic review by the ARC or a state‑level Economic Advisory Council to align welfare spending with income‑generation strategies.
    • Leverage central schemes (e.g., PM Gati Shakti) to improve connectivity and attract manufacturing.

Prelims Pointers

  • Access Equality Index – composite measure of service delivery across five sectors.
  • Per‑capita income ranking – indicator of economic disparity among states.
  • Constitutional provisions: Article 41 (right to work), Article 46 (promotion of educational & economic interests of weaker sections).
  • Relevant bodies: NITI Aayog (index compilation), ARC (policy review), State Planning Commission.
