‘XLRI’s emphasis on values, responsible leadership has been my touchstone’

20 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: XLRI alumnus Madhavi Lall, now MD & HR Head of Deutsche Bank India, highlighted how the institute’s focus on values and responsible leadership shaped her professional ethos. The statement underscores the growing relevance of value‑based management education for corporate governance in India.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper I – Education, Human Resource Development; GS Paper II – Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude; GS Paper III – Economic Development (Corporate Governance)

Key Data & Stats: XLRI founded 1949 Deutsche Bank India operations since 1995

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Post‑liberalisation, Indian management institutes expanded their curricula to include ethics, sustainability and stakeholder‑centric leadership, aligning with global ESG trends and the National Education Policy 2020’s emphasis on value‑based education.
  • Significance: Graduates imbibing responsible leadership can strengthen corporate governance, improve employee welfare, and enhance India’s ESG credentials—critical for attracting foreign investment and meeting SEBI’s recent ESG disclosure mandates.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: A gap often exists between classroom teachings on ethics and real‑world corporate practices; lack of a standardized ethics module across AICTE‑approved management programmes and limited monitoring by statutory bodies can dilute impact.
  • Way Forward: • Integrate a compulsory “Ethics & Responsible Leadership” module accredited by AICTE and UGC.
    • Periodic audits of alumni‑led CSR/ESG initiatives by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.
    • Set up a joint committee (including members of the ARC and the National Knowledge Commission) to review and update value‑based curricula every five years.

  • XLRI – Xavier School of Management, Jamshedpur (est. 1949)
  • National Education Policy 2020: emphasis on value‑based and ethical education
  • Corporate Governance & ESG disclosures – SEBI (2022) guidelines
  • Role of HR in fostering ethical culture – relevant to GS II (Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude)
