Zelensky holds air defence, energy security talks with Nordic leaders

10 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky met Nordic leaders in Oslo at the end of October to discuss strengthening Ukraine’s air‑defence capabilities and enhancing energy‑security cooperation. The Nordic bloc, a key European supporter of Kyiv, hosted the talks alongside American envoys.
