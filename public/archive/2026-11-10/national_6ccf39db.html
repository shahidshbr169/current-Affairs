Guntur DRO calls for accuracy, coordination among officials during Census 2027 exercise

10 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The District Registrar Officer (DRO) of Guntur, N.S.K. Khajavali, emphasized the necessity for precise data collection and seamless coordination among officials during the upcoming Census 2027. He urged field officers to internalise the procedural guidelines taught in training to ensure the integrity of the enumeration process.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper I – Indian Society & Social Justice; GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (Statutory bodies, Census of India Act, 1948); GS Paper III – Technology & Development (use of digital tools in census).

Key Data & Stats: Census 2027 Census of India Act, 1948 Decennial Census (every 10 years)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The Census of India, a constitutionally mandated decennial exercise, is conducted under the Census of India Act, 1948 and overseen by the Ministry of Home Affairs. The 2027 round follows the 2011 and 2021 censuses (the latter postponed due to the pandemic).
  • Significance: Accurate census data underpins policy formulation, allocation of central assistance, delimitation of constituencies, and planning of welfare schemes. Errors can distort demographic indicators, affecting fiscal transfers and electoral representation.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Inadequate field‑level training, fragmented coordination among multiple agencies, and the transition to digital enumeration raise risks of data gaps and privacy concerns. Remote and tribal areas pose logistical hurdles, while the pressure to meet timelines may compromise data quality.
  • Way Forward: • Strengthen the training module with mock drills and GIS‑based simulations.
    • Institutionalise a joint monitoring cell comprising the Ministry of Home Affairs, National Statistical Office (NSO) and State Statistical Departments (ARC‑style oversight).
    • Deploy encrypted mobile applications for real‑time data capture and validation.
    • Periodic audits by the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) to ensure compliance with the Census Act.

Prelims Pointers

  • Decennial Census – conducted every 10 years under the Census of India Act, 1948.
  • District Registrar Officer (DRO) – key nodal officer for field‑level census operations.
  • Ministry of Home Affairs – custodial authority for census administration.
  • Use of digital enumeration tools – a recent shift aimed at improving accuracy and speed.
