JNTUA to host ‘Viksit Bharat Youth Parliament’ on November 12

10 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Anantapur (JNTUA) will organise the ‘Viksit Bharat Youth Parliament’ on 12 Nov. The event aims to simulate parliamentary proceedings for students, fostering democratic engagement and policy‑making skills among youth.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; GS Paper III – Science & Technology, Development

Key Data & Stats: 12 Nov 2024 (event date) | ~2,000 (estimated student participants, based on previous editions) | Article 19(1)(a) (Freedom of speech & expression)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Youth Parliaments have been promoted by the Ministry of Education and various state governments to inculcate parliamentary etiquette and civic responsibility. JNTUA’s ‘Viksit Bharat’ series aligns with the National Education Policy 2020’s emphasis on experiential learning.
  • Significance: Enhances democratic literacy, creates a pipeline of informed future leaders, and provides a platform for youth to deliberate on national issues such as skill development, employment, and sustainable development – directly relevant to the “Skill India” and “Digital India” missions.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Limited outreach beyond engineering colleges, risk of partisan bias, and lack of formal linkage with policy‑making bodies may dilute impact. Ensuring representation from marginalized groups and rural youth remains a bottleneck.
  • Way Forward: • Institutionalise the Youth Parliament under the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports with periodic evaluation by the **National Institution for Transforming India (NITI Aayog)**.
    • Adopt a multi‑stakeholder model involving State Youth Welfare Boards, NGOs, and academic institutions.
    • Recommend a **Committee on Youth Civic Engagement** (modeled on the Sarkaria Commission) to draft guidelines for inclusivity, funding, and integration of outcomes into policy drafts.
