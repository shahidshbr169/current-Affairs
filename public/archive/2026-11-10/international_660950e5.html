U.S.-mediated talks on ending war between Russia, Ukraine begin in Abu Dhabi

10 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: The United States facilitated a two‑day diplomatic track in Abu Dhabi, marking the first Russia‑Ukraine peace talks since February. Delegates will concentrate on formulating security guarantees to halt hostilities.
