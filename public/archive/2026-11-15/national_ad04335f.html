Proposal to set up new KSISF battalion in Kalaburagi; 1,170 posts envisaged

15 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The Karnataka government has proposed raising a new Karnataka State Industrial Security Force (KSISF) battalion at Kalaburagi with 1,170 posts to strengthen security in the Kalyana Karnataka region. The move aims to reduce the reliance on the existing 2nd and 3rd KSISF battalions located 245 km (Shivamogga) and 350 km (Dharwad) away, thereby improving response time and local employment.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; GS Paper III – Internal Security, Law & Order

Key Data & Stats: 1,170 posts, 245 km (Shivamogga), 350 km (Dharwad)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: KSISF, a state‑run paramilitary force, currently secures industrial and strategic installations in Karnataka through three battalions. The Kalyana Karnataka region, historically under‑served, depends on battalions stationed far away, affecting operational efficiency.
  • Significance: A locally‑based battalion will cut down deployment time, enhance protection of critical infrastructure, and generate 1,170 direct jobs, contributing to regional development and social stability.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Funding allocation, recruitment and training of personnel, integration with the state police, and clear demarcation of jurisdiction are potential bottlenecks. Legal clarity on the command‑structure under the Home Department versus the Police Department may be required.
  • Way Forward: • Set up an inter‑departmental committee (Home, Finance, Police) to finalize budget and recruitment plan.
    • Seek guidance from the Ministry of Home Affairs on standards for state paramilitary expansion.
    • Periodic review by a committee akin to the Sarkaria Commission to ensure federal‑state coordination and avoid overlap with central forces.
    • Implement a phased deployment with performance audits after 12 months.

Prelims Pointers

  • KSISF – Karnataka State Industrial Security Force (state paramilitary under Home Department).
  • Kalyana Karnataka – former “Hyderabad Karnataka” region; a backward area receiving special central & state schemes.
  • State subjects on law & order and internal security are listed in List II of the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution.
  • New battalion to be raised with 1,170 posts at Kalaburagi.
