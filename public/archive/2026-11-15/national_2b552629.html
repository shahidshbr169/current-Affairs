BJP MP writes to USCIRF, says Sharjeel in jail for alleged role in Delhi riots, not faith

15 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: A BJP MP wrote to the US Commission on International Religious Freedom (USCIRF) asserting that Sharjeel Imam is incarcerated for his alleged involvement in the Delhi riots, not on the basis of his faith. The USCIRF, in its 2026 report, has designated India as a “Country of Particular Concern” (CPC) on religious‑freedom grounds and highlighted Imam’s case on its platforms.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; International Relations (US‑India diplomatic interface); Human Rights & Fundamental Rights.

Key Data & Stats: 2026 USCIRF Report Country of Particular Concern (CPC) designation Sharjeel Imam – detained for alleged Delhi riots role

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: USCIRF annually reviews global religious‑freedom conditions and can label a nation as CPC when “systemic, ongoing, and egregious violations” are evident. India’s designation follows concerns over post‑2020 Delhi riots, the arrest of activist Sharjeel Imam, and broader reports of communal tensions.
  • Significance: The CPC tag can affect India’s diplomatic image, invite scrutiny in multilateral forums, and influence foreign aid or trade considerations. Domestically, it fuels debate on the balance between law‑and‑order actions against alleged rioters and the protection of Article 25‑26 freedoms.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis:
    • Ensuring due‑process rights while addressing communal violence.
    • Potential perception of selective enforcement against minority leaders.
    • Risk of diplomatic friction with the United States and other democracies that monitor CPC designations.
    • Need for transparent criteria linking specific cases (e.g., Imam’s detention) to broader religious‑freedom assessments.
  • Way Forward:
    • Set up an independent review committee (modeled on the ARC) to examine allegations of religious‑freedom violations linked to law‑enforcement actions.
    • Strengthen judicial oversight of preventive detention and ensure compliance with Article 22(2) of the Constitution.
    • Engage constructively with USCIRF through diplomatic channels, providing detailed case files and corrective action plans.
    • Enhance community‑level conflict‑prevention mechanisms and promote inter‑faith dialogue to mitigate recurrence of communal riots.

Prelims Pointers

  • USCIRF – US Commission on International Religious Freedom, statutory body that can label nations as CPC.
  • Country of Particular Concern (CPC) – a designation under the International Religious Freedom Act, 1998.
  • Article 25 & 26, Constitution of India – guarantee freedom of conscience, religion, and right to manage religious affairs.
  • Sharjeel Imam – activist arrested for alleged role in the 2020 Delhi riots; case cited by USCIRF.
