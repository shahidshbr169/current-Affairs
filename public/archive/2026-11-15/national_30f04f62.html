India, Pakistan And New Delhi SCO Meet After Op Sindoor: 10 Things To Know Amid Strained Ties

15 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: India and Pakistan met on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Summit in New Delhi, marking the first high‑level dialogue after the “Op Sindoor” diplomatic row. The encounter underscores the SCO’s role as a platform for regional confidence‑building despite bilateral tensions.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – International Relations, Bilateral Treaties & Regional Organisations; GS Paper III – Security Challenges & Diplomacy

Key Data & Stats: 10‑member Eurasian bloc annual Council of Heads of State meeting

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The SCO, founded in 2001, brings together China, Russia, Central Asian states and India & Pakistan (joined 2017). “Op Sindoor” (April 2024) saw India recall its ambassador over alleged Pakistani interference, straining ties.
  • Significance: The meeting demonstrates how multilateral fora can provide neutral ground for conflict‑prone neighbours to engage, preserving the SCO’s cohesion and India’s strategic autonomy in Eurasia.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Persistent mistrust, divergent security perceptions (especially on terrorism and border incidents), and the absence of a binding dispute‑resolution mechanism limit substantive outcomes.
  • Way Forward: Institutionalise a SCO‑level confidence‑building mechanism (e.g., regular India‑Pakistan track under the SCO Secretariat). A joint expert committee (modeled on the Indo‑Pak Joint Working Group) could address border incidents, while the ARC‑style review could assess the efficacy of SCO’s conflict‑management provisions.

Prelims Pointers

  • Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) – 10‑member Eurasian regional bloc.
  • Council of Heads of State – highest decision‑making organ of SCO, meets annually.
  • “Op Sindoor” – diplomatic row (April 2024) leading to recall of Indian ambassador from Pakistan.
  • India & Pakistan’s participation in SCO since 2017 provides a multilateral platform for dialogue.
