Asean BAC highlights its role in scaling up MSMEs

15 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: The ASEAN Business and Advisory Council (BAC) chaired by Jose Maria Concepcion concluded the ASEAN Business and Investment Summit in Manila, reaffirming its role as a conduit between governments and the private sector to accelerate the growth of micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) across the region.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Bilateral, regional and global groupings involving India (ASEAN, ASEAN‑India strategic partnership); GS Paper III – Role of MSMEs in economic development.

Key Data & Agreements: ASEAN Business and Advisory Council (BAC) • ASEAN Business and Investment Summit, Manila 2024 • Commitment to bridge government‑business dialogue for MSME scaling

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: Strengthening ASEAN’s intra‑regional business ecosystem enhances the bloc’s collective bargaining power in global supply chains, offering a counter‑balance to China’s dominance and creating a more diversified market for foreign investment.
  • Impact on India: A more robust ASEAN MSME sector expands opportunities for Indian firms in sectors such as pharmaceuticals, IT services, and renewable energy, while also deepening India’s strategic outreach under the “Act East” policy and the ASEAN‑India Free Trade Area.
  • Way Forward: India should leverage the BAC platform to negotiate MSME‑focused trade facilitation measures, promote joint incubation programmes, and align financing mechanisms (e.g., ADB‑backed credit lines) with Indian development banks to tap the ASEAN MSME market.

Prelims Pointers

  • ASEAN Business and Advisory Council (BAC) – a key private‑sector advisory body to ASEAN.
  • 2024 ASEAN Business and Investment Summit held in Manila, Philippines.
  • Focus on scaling MSMEs – critical for employment generation and regional economic resilience.
  • India’s “Act East” policy seeks deeper economic integration with ASEAN, especially in the MSME segment.
