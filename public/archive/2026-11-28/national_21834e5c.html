‘India is a great power, whose rise we will continue to witness’: Danish FM as 5-year action plan adopted

28 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Denmark and India have adopted a 5‑year joint action plan to deepen bilateral cooperation. The Danish FM hailed India as a rising great power, and the detailed roadmap will be disclosed when the two Prime Ministers meet.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper III – International Relations, Bilateral Treaties & Strategic Partnerships; GS Paper II – Governance & Foreign Policy

Key Data & Stats: 5‑year action plan, PM‑to‑PM meeting pending

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: India and Denmark have a long‑standing diplomatic relationship dating back to 1947, with cooperation in renewable energy, maritime safety, Arctic research, and defence. The recent plan builds on the 2022 “Strategic Partnership” framework.
  • Significance: Formalising a multi‑year action plan signals India’s growing strategic clout in Europe and aligns with its “Act East” and “Neighbourhood First” doctrines. It opens avenues for technology transfer in green energy, enhances maritime cooperation in the Indian Ocean, and diversifies India’s diplomatic outreach beyond traditional partners.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Implementation may be hampered by differing regulatory standards, financing gaps, and the need to harmonise Denmark’s EU‑centric policies with India’s domestic priorities. Coordination between multiple ministries (External Affairs, Commerce, Energy, Defence) requires robust inter‑ministerial mechanisms.
  • Way Forward: • Institutionalise a Joint Working Group chaired by the Ministry of External Affairs and NITI Aayog for periodic review.
    • Adopt a “one‑stop‑shop” clearance mechanism for projects under the plan, akin to the ARC‑recommended fast‑track for strategic agreements.
    • Schedule the PM‑to‑PM summit within the next six months to finalise sector‑specific MoUs.
    • Leverage existing platforms such as the Indo‑European Union Dialogue for funding and technical assistance.

Prelims Pointers

  • India‑Denmark strategic partnership – focus on renewable energy, maritime safety, and defence cooperation.
  • 5‑year joint action plan adopted to operationalise bilateral commitments.
  • Key diplomatic channel – upcoming Prime Ministerial meeting to unveil detailed roadmap.
  • Relevance to UPSC: International relations, bilateral treaties, and strategic autonomy of India.
