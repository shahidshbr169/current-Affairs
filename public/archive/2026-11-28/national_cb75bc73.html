Outdated courses could leave graduates jobless, warns CM

28 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Kerala Chief Minister P. Satheesan warned that graduates pursuing outdated courses risk unemployment, urging the state to realign higher‑education curricula with emerging employment trends to keep the youth job‑ready.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper III – Economic Development, Employment, Poverty; GS Paper II – Governance, Education Policy, Public Administration

Key Data & Stats: Data not disclosed in the release

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The National Education Policy 2020 (NEP‑2020) emphasizes multidisciplinary, skill‑oriented curricula. Persistent skill‑gap reports and rising graduate‑unemployment ratios have prompted state leaders to revisit course relevance.
  • Significance: Aligning academic programmes with market demand enhances human capital, curbs educated unemployment, and contributes to higher per‑capita income—key indicators for inclusive growth.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Institutional inertia of universities, fragmented accreditation (UGC, AICTE), lack of real‑time labour‑market data, and limited industry‑academia collaboration impede swift curriculum overhaul.
  • Way Forward: • Institutionalise periodic curriculum review through a state‑level “Curriculum Alignment Committee” modeled on the ARC recommendations.
    • Strengthen Industry‑Academia Partnership Cells (IAPCs) under the Skill Development Mission.
    • Leverage NEP‑2020 provisions for outcome‑based education and introduce “Future Skills” modules.
    • Use data‑analytics platforms (e.g., NITI‑Aayog’s Skill Gap Index) for evidence‑based course redesign.

Prelims Pointers

  • National Education Policy 2020 – focus on multidisciplinary, skill‑oriented higher education.
  • Article 21A of the Constitution – Right to Education (relevant for policy reforms in higher education).
  • UGC (University Grants Commission) & AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) – statutory bodies governing curricula and accreditation.
  • Skill Development Mission – central scheme for aligning education with employability.
