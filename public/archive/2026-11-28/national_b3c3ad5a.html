Thirupparankundram row LIVE: Karthigai Deepam lit at 'deepathoon'; Supreme Court- appointed receivers oversee the ceremony

28 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: The Supreme Court found that the state executive deliberately flouted its earlier directions by not lighting the ceremonial Karthigai Deepam at the contested stone pillar in Thirupparankundram. Consequently, the Court has appointed receivers to supervise the lighting ceremony, underscoring judicial enforcement of its orders.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (Judicial review, executive accountability, Article 32, statutory bodies)

Key Data & Stats: Supreme Court order Executive wilful disobedience Receivers appointed

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The stone pillar at Thirupparankundram is a heritage site that has been the subject of a long‑standing dispute between the state government and local religious groups over its ownership and the right to perform the annual Karthigai Deepam ritual.
  • Significance: The episode highlights the limits of executive discretion when it contravenes a Supreme Court directive, reinforcing the principle of judicial supremacy and the rule of law in administrative actions.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Persistent executive non‑compliance erodes public confidence in constitutional mechanisms; the ad‑hoc appointment of receivers raises questions about the adequacy of existing enforcement tools for Supreme Court orders.
  • Way Forward: Strengthen mechanisms for compliance, e.g., empower the Supreme Court to impose monetary penalties for contempt; consider a review committee (similar to the Sarkaria Commission) to examine executive‑judicial coordination in heritage‑related disputes; institutionalise a monitoring cell within the Ministry of Law & Justice for timely implementation of court orders.

Prelims Pointers

  • Supreme Court’s power of judicial review under Article 32 of the Constitution.
  • Executive’s duty to comply with court orders – contempt of court provisions.
  • Receivership as an extraordinary remedial measure to enforce compliance.
  • Karthigai Deepam – annual cultural‑religious ceremony linked to heritage management.
