Shivamogga MP calls upon youths to understand basic principles of Indian Constitution

28 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: The Shivamogga MP appealed to the youth to acquaint themselves with the basic tenets of the Indian Constitution, emphasizing that constitutional literacy is essential for responsible citizenship and effective participation in democratic processes.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity

Key Data & Stats: N/A

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Persistent gaps in civic knowledge among Indian youth have been highlighted by surveys (e.g., Lok Sabha’s 2022 youth poll) and by the Ministry of Education’s reports on the limited coverage of constitutional education in schools.
  • Significance: A constitution‑aware youth base strengthens democratic accountability, improves voter turnout, and curtails the spread of misinformation, thereby reinforcing the rule of law and federal harmony.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Inadequate integration of civics in the school curriculum, lack of teacher training, language barriers in rural areas, and limited use of digital platforms hinder widespread constitutional awareness.
  • Way Forward: • Incorporate a structured “Constitution Literacy” module in the NCERT curriculum as per the National Education Policy‑2020.
    • Launch a periodic “Constitution Day” outreach programme under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, leveraging NGOs and university bodies.
    • Set up a multi‑stakeholder committee (including members of the Sarkaria Commission) to monitor implementation and suggest periodic revisions.
    • Promote e‑learning tools and regional language resources to reach diverse linguistic groups.
