DMK MPs to sit apart from INDIA bloc in Lok Sabha as party backs delimitation Bills

28 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: DMK MPs will sit separately from the INDIA bloc in the Lok Sabha after the party announced its support for the revised delimitation Bills. The move is expected to help the Centre secure the two‑thirds majority needed to pass the Bills in Parliament.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (Delimitation, Article 82 & 170, constitutional amendment procedure, party politics & coalition dynamics)

Key Data & Stats: Delimitation Bills (2024), Two‑thirds majority required under Article 368, DMK – regional party in Tamil Nadu, INDIA bloc – opposition alliance

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The Delimitation Commission, constituted under Article 82 of the Constitution, last undertook a nationwide exercise in 2002. A constitutional freeze on seat‑reallocation was imposed by the 84th Amendment (2001) until after the 2026 Census. The revised Bills aim to lift the freeze and redraw constituencies based on the 2021 Census, prompting intense political bargaining.
  • Significance: Passage of the Bills will reshape the electoral map, affecting representation of states, especially those with changing demographics. For the Centre, securing a two‑thirds majority is crucial to avoid a constitutional amendment hurdle. DMK’s separate seating signals a strategic shift that could alter coalition arithmetic and impact federal balance.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis:
    • Potential legal challenges on the basis of the 84th Amendment’s freeze clause.
    • Political resistance from states fearing loss of seats (e.g., Uttar Pradesh, Bihar) versus gains for high‑growth states (e.g., Tamil Nadu, Kerala).
    • Risk of communal or regional polarization if delimitation is perceived as partisan.
  • Way Forward:
    • Form a bipartisan parliamentary committee (modeled on the Sarkaria Commission) to review the delimitation methodology and ensure transparency.
    • Empower the Delimitation Commission with clear, time‑bound guidelines and a provision for judicial review to pre‑empt litigation.
    • Engage state governments through the Inter‑State Council to address concerns of seat redistribution, preserving the spirit of cooperative federalism.

Prelims Pointers

  • Delimitation Bill – aims to lift the freeze on constituency re‑allocation (Article 82, Constitution of India).
  • Article 368 – constitutional amendment requires a two‑thirds majority of total members of both Houses.
  • DMK (Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam) – regional party in Tamil Nadu, part of the opposition but now sitting apart from the INDIA bloc.
  • INDIA alliance – coalition of opposition parties formed ahead of 2024 Lok Sabha elections.
