Moment Chinese space mission lifts off with first Pakistan astronaut aboard

25 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: China’s Shenzhou‑24 mission lifted off, carrying three crew members to the Tiangong space station, including Pakistan’s first astronaut. The flight underscores deepening Sino‑Pakistani space cooperation amid intensifying great‑power competition.

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The launch signals China’s expanding soft power through joint space ventures, positioning itself as a technology partner for developing nations and challenging the US‑led space dominance.
  • Impact on India: Strengthens China‑Pakistan strategic ties, potentially widening the Indo‑Pak space and defence gap; compels India to accelerate its own civilian‑military space collaborations and articulate a clear stance on dual‑use technology transfers.
  • Way Forward: India should deepen engagement with multilateral space forums (e.g., UN COPUOS), explore joint missions with friendly nations, and develop a robust policy on technology sharing to safeguard strategic autonomy while leveraging commercial opportunities.
