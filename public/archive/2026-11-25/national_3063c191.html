APPSC reschedules assistant professors’, RGUKT lecturers’ exams to December 20-24

25 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: The Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) has shifted the examination dates for Assistant Professors and RGUKT Lecturers to December 20‑24. The rescheduling is attributed to administrative reasons, with a detailed subject‑wise timetable to be issued later.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (State Public Service Commissions, recruitment & service conditions)

Key Data & Stats: December 20‑24 (new exam window)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: APPSC conducts periodic recruitment for higher‑education posts in Andhra Pradesh, including Assistant Professors and lecturers for the RGUKT network. The original schedule was altered due to unspecified administrative constraints.
  • Significance: Timely recruitment ensures continuity in higher‑education delivery, impacts faculty‑student ratios, and reflects the efficiency of state‑level recruitment machinery mandated under Article 315 of the Constitution.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Unexplained schedule changes can lead to candidate uncertainty, potential legal challenges under the APPSC Act, and may affect the academic calendar of institutions awaiting new faculty.
  • Way Forward: • Adopt a transparent, calendar‑based exam schedule published well in advance.
    • Strengthen internal audit mechanisms within APPSC to pre‑empt administrative bottlenecks.
    • Consider periodic oversight by a state‑level committee (e.g., a review panel under the Department of Higher Education) to ensure adherence to statutory timelines.

Prelims Pointers

  • APPSC – State Public Service Commission (Article 315, Constitution of India).
  • RGUKT – Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies, a network of autonomous institutions.
  • Assistant Professor recruitment – governed by University Grants Commission (UGC) norms and state service rules.
  • Exam rescheduling – reflects administrative discretion but must align with statutory timelines for recruitment.
