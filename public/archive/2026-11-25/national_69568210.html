Karnataka High Court orders fresh FIRs as Enforcement Directorate probe discloses illegalities in KPSC’s four more recruitment exams

25 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The Karnataka High Court has ordered police to file fresh FIRs wherever the Enforcement Directorate’s probe uncovers cognisable offences in four additional recruitment examinations conducted by the Karnataka Public Service Commission (KPSC). The directive underscores judicial vigilance over alleged corruption in state recruitment processes.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; GS Paper III – Ethics, Integrity and Aptitude (Public Administration)

Key Data & Stats: four recruitment exams Karnataka High Court order Enforcement Directorate (ED) probe

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: State Public Service Commissions (PSC) are constitutional bodies under Article 315(1) tasked with merit‑based recruitment for state services. Over the past few years, multiple states have faced allegations of paper‑leakage, favouritism, and financial irregularities in PSC exams, prompting ED investigations and judicial interventions.
  • Significance: The order reinforces the principle of accountability of statutory recruitment agencies, safeguards the merit‑based ethos of civil services, and signals that any breach of the Public Procurement and Corruption Prevention frameworks will attract criminal prosecution.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis:
    • Limited oversight mechanisms for PSCs; existing checks (e.g., internal audit, vigilance) often lack real‑time monitoring.
    • Potential conflict between state autonomy (Article 315) and central investigative agencies (ED) in probing state‑level recruitment.
    • Risk of prolonged litigation affecting the timely filling of vacancies, thereby impacting public service delivery.
  • Way Forward:
    • Constitution of an independent oversight committee (modeled on the Sarkaria Commission) to review PSC recruitment protocols and recommend digitalisation of exam processes.
    • Mandate periodic compliance audits by the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) for all state PSCs.
    • Introduce a statutory “Recruitment Integrity Act” at the centre‑state level, prescribing penalties for tampering with exam papers and ensuring swift adjudication of FIRs.
    • Strengthen inter‑agency coordination between the High Courts, ED, and State Vigilance Departments through a MoU for fast‑track investigations.

Prelims Pointers

  • Article 315(1) – Constitution establishes State Public Service Commissions.
  • Karnataka Public Service Commission (KPSC) – statutory body for recruitment to state services.
  • Enforcement Directorate (ED) – central agency investigating money‑laundering and financial crimes.
  • High Court’s power under CrPC to direct registration of FIRs for cognisable offences.
