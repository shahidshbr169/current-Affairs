Kannada writer Ravi Hanj arrested at Kochi airport amid Basava Mantapa book controversy

21 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Kannada writer Ravi Hanj was detained by Kerala police at Kochi airport during security checks while attempting to travel abroad. He is being handed over to Karnataka police in connection with the ongoing controversy over his book “Basava Mantapa”.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; GS Paper III – Internal Security & Law & Order; GS Paper I – Society & Culture (Freedom of Expression)

Key Data & Stats: “Basava Mantapa” book controversy | Article 19(1)(a) – Freedom of Speech & Expression | Article 21 – Right to Life & Personal Liberty | Section 124A IPC – Sedition (potential charge)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The book “Basava Mantapa” has sparked protests from certain cultural and religious groups in Karnataka, alleging distortion of historical/religious sentiments. Earlier, similar works have led to FIRs, bans, and arrests, prompting heightened police vigilance.
  • Significance: The incident underscores the tension between freedom of expression and public order, inter‑state police coordination, and the role of law‑enforcement agencies in handling culturally sensitive material.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: • Jurisdictional ambiguity – Kerala police detained the writer, but the alleged offence pertains to Karnataka. • Potential misuse of sedition provisions – balancing curbing hate speech with protecting legitimate dissent. • Procedural safeguards – ensuring the writer’s right to personal liberty (Art. 21) and speedy hand‑over.
  • Way Forward: • Formulate a clear inter‑state protocol under the CrPC for handing over persons arrested for offences relating to cultural sensitivities. • Law Commission to review the applicability of Section 124A IPC in cases of literary expression. • State governments to set up a fast‑track committee (similar to the Sarkaria Commission) to mediate cultural‑sensitivity disputes without resorting to criminal prosecution.

Prelims Pointers

  • Article 19(1)(a) – Right to Freedom of Speech & Expression.
  • Article 21 – Protection of Life & Personal Liberty; due‑process clause.
  • Section 124A IPC – Sedition law, often invoked in cultural‑sensitivity cases.
  • Inter‑state police cooperation under the Criminal Procedure Code (CrPC) and the Police Act, 1861.
