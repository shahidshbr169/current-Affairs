Revanth to inaugurate Poultry India Expo in Hyderabad on Nov. 25

21 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Actor‑producer Revanth will inaugurate the Poultry India Expo in Hyderabad on Nov 25, 2026. The expo gathers manufacturers, farmers, and policymakers to showcase innovations and discuss growth strategies for India’s poultry sector, a key component of the nation’s food‑security and export agenda.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper III – Agriculture, Food Processing & Allied Sectors; GS Paper II – Governance & Policy (Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, statutory bodies)

Key Data & Stats: Indian poultry industry turnover ≈ ₹1.5 lakh crore (≈ $20 bn), ≈ 70 % of total meat consumption, ≈ 1.2 crore livestock, Export earnings ≈ ₹30 billion (FY 2025‑26)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Over the last decade, India has emerged as the world’s third‑largest poultry producer, driven by rising disposable incomes, urbanisation, and government initiatives such as the National Livestock Mission (NLM) and PM‑Kisan subsidies for small‑scale farmers.
  • Significance: The sector provides direct employment to ≈ 1.5 million workers, contributes to nutritional security (protein), and earns valuable foreign exchange through exports to the Middle East, Southeast Asia and Africa.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Bio‑security lapses (e.g., avian influenza), fragmented supply chains, limited cold‑storage infrastructure, and regulatory gaps in feed safety and animal welfare pose risks to sustainable growth.
  • Way Forward: • Strengthen the role of the National Centre for Animal Health (NCAH) for disease surveillance.
    • Accelerate implementation of NLM components on farm‑gate credit and cold‑chain logistics.
    • Form a multi‑stakeholder task‑force (Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, FSSAI, State Animal Husbandry Departments) to standardise feed quality and animal‑welfare norms.
    • Encourage Public‑Private Partnerships for technology transfer in vaccine development and precision farming.

Prelims Pointers

  • Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying (MoFA) – nodal agency for poultry policy.
  • National Livestock Mission (NLM) – flagship scheme for livestock development (incl. poultry).
  • PM‑Kisan Scheme – provides credit-linked subsidy to marginal poultry farmers.
  • Export‑oriented growth: India’s poultry exports rank 3rd globally, major markets include Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Singapore.
