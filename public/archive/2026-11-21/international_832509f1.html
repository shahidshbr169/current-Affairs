Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The convergence of the Quad, ASEAN‑India and East Asia forums in a single venue underscores a coordinated effort to shape the Indo‑Pacific order, counterbalancing unilateral moves by other great powers.
