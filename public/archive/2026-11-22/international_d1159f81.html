‘Two rulers, one fort’: Pakistan Minister invokes Humayun, Sher Shah at SCO Civilisational Forum

22 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: At the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Civilisational Forum held in Delhi’s Purana Qila, Pakistan’s Minister Musadik Malik highlighted the fort’s historic links to Mughal emperor Humayun and Afghan ruler Sher Shah Suri, underscoring shared Central‑Asian heritage among SCO members.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Bilateral, regional and global groupings involving India (SCO, cultural diplomacy, India‑Pakistan relations).

Key Data & Agreements: SCO Civilisational Forum 2024 – Delhi (Purana Qila)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The forum reflects SCO’s expanding agenda beyond security to cultural and civilisational dialogue, aiming to foster a common narrative that links member states to a shared Central‑Asian past, thereby strengthening intra‑regional cohesion.
  • Impact on India: India can leverage the cultural platform to project its own civilisational legacy, counterbalance Pakistan’s narrative, and deepen people‑to‑people ties with Central Asian SCO members, enhancing soft‑power leverage in a grouping traditionally dominated by China and Russia.
  • Way Forward: India should institutionalise cultural tracks within SCO, promote joint heritage projects (e.g., museum exchanges, archaeological collaborations), and use the narrative to advance its “Act East” and “Neighbourhood First” policies while maintaining strategic autonomy.
