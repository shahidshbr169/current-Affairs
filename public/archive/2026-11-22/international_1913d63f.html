Myanmar calls ASEAN review of peace plan 'unfair and imbalanced'; questions bloc's treatment

22 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Myanmar’s Foreign Ministry criticised the ASEAN‑mandated review of the bloc’s peace plan, calling it “unfair and imbalanced” and arguing that the recommendations adopted at the recent ASEAN Summit in Manila do not acknowledge the positive steps taken by Myanmar.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Regional groupings and organisations (ASEAN), India’s neighbourhood policy, and issues of regional security and conflict resolution.

Key Data & Agreements: ASEAN Summit, Manila (2024) – Review of ASEAN Peace Plan for Myanmar

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The dissent underscores the fragility of ASEAN’s consensus‑based mechanism in conflict zones, potentially weakening the bloc’s credibility as a mediator in the Indo‑Pacific and inviting external powers to fill the diplomatic vacuum.
  • Impact on India: Instability in Myanmar affects India’s northeastern states, cross‑border trade, and the security of the India‑Myanmar–Thailand Trilateral Highway; it also raises concerns over the safety of the sizable Indian diaspora and the flow of insurgent groups.
  • Way Forward: India can deepen engagement through the “Act East” framework, promote a balanced ASEAN‑Myanmar dialogue, offer technical assistance for confidence‑building measures, and coordinate with like‑minded partners (Japan, Australia, USA) to sustain a neutral mediation role.

Prelims Pointers

  • ASEAN’s principle of non‑interference vs. collective security mandates.
  • Manila Declaration (2024) – adoption of revised peace‑plan recommendations for Myanmar.
  • India’s “Act East” policy and its strategic interest in a stable Myanmar.
  • Potential involvement of external powers (China, USA) in Myanmar’s peace process.
