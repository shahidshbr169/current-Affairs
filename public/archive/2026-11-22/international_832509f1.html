Key takeaways from the Quad, ASEAN-India, and East Asia Summits in Manila | Explained

22 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: The recent Quad, ASEAN‑India and East Asia Summits held in Manila underscored India’s push to deepen engagement with ASEAN and other regional groupings amid a backdrop of increasing unilateral actions by major powers.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Bilateral, regional and global groupings involving India; International Relations; India’s foreign policy and strategic autonomy.

Key Data & Agreements: Manila, Philippines – 2024, Quad members: US, Japan, Australia, India, East Asia Summit – 18 member states, ASEAN‑India Strategic Partnership – 2024 reaffirmation

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The convergence of Quad and ASEAN‑India dialogues in Manila signals a coordinated effort to balance China’s assertiveness in the Indo‑Pacific while promoting a rules‑based order.
  • Impact on India: Strengthened ties with ASEAN enhance India’s economic outreach (trade, connectivity) and provide strategic depth through maritime cooperation, complementing Quad’s security framework.
  • Way Forward: India should institutionalise regular Quad‑ASEAN consultations, accelerate infrastructure projects under the Asia‑Africa Growth Corridor, and leverage the East Asia Summit to champion climate‑security linkages.

Prelims Pointers

  • Quad – a strategic dialogue of the US, Japan, Australia and India focusing on a free, open, inclusive Indo‑Pacific.
  • ASEAN‑India Summit 2024 held in Manila reaffirmed the Strategic Partnership and discussed connectivity, trade and maritime security.
  • East Asia Summit comprises 18 countries, providing a platform for broader regional security and economic cooperation.
  • Manila as the host city reflects the Philippines’ growing role as a facilitator of Indo‑Pacific multilateralism.
