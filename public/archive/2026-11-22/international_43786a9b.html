Zelensky says Crimean Bridge strikes 'fair response' as he meets UK's Streeting

22 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky described the recent strikes on the Crimean Bridge as a “fair response” after Kyiv retaliated for 109 separate Russian aerial attacks on Ukrainian bridges. He made the remarks while meeting the United Kingdom’s Foreign Secretary, David Streeting.

Deep-Dive Analysis
