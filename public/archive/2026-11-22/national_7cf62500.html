Police tighten security across Rayalaseema districts ahead of Panchayat polls

22 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Police have intensified security in the Rayalaseema districts of Chittoor, Annamayya and Sri Sathya Sai ahead of the upcoming Panchayat elections, deploying drones, conducting flag marches and closely monitoring sensitive villages to avert clashes and electoral violence.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; GS Paper III – Internal Security, Law & Order; GS Paper I – Indian Society (rural‑political dynamics)

Key Data & Stats: 3 districts Panchayat polls 2024 Use of UAVs for election security

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Rayalaseema has a long‑standing history of factional politics and episodic electoral violence. The State Election Commission has scheduled Panchayat elections to renew grassroots democratic institutions, prompting heightened security measures.
  • Significance: Ensuring a peaceful polling environment safeguards the integrity of the Panchayati Raj system (Article 243K, 243R) and prevents disruption of rural development programmes. It also reflects the state’s capacity to manage law‑and‑order challenges during mass electoral exercises.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: • Terrain and dispersed habitations make surveillance difficult.
    • Political rivalries may trigger flashpoints in “sensitive villages”.
    • Deployment of drones raises privacy and data‑security concerns; clear SOPs are often lacking.
    • Coordination between police, State Election Commission and local bodies can be fragmented.
  • Way Forward: • Form a joint task‑force headed by the State Election Commission with police, district administration and civil‑society representatives (as recommended by the Sarkaria Commission on law‑and‑order coordination).
    • Institutionalise SOPs for UAV usage, including data‑retention limits and civilian oversight.
    • Intensify community‑policing initiatives and grievance redressal mechanisms in sensitive villages.
    • Conduct pre‑poll sensitisation camps for political parties on the Model Code of Conduct and legal consequences of violence.

Prelims Pointers

  • Article 243K – Power of State Election Commission to conduct Panchayat elections.
  • Model Code of Conduct (MCC) – enforcement during local body polls.
  • Use of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) for election security – emerging best practice.
  • Rayalaseema – region noted for factionalism; relevance to law‑and‑order management.
