Pakistan’s participation in SCO Civilisational Forum | Explained

16 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: India will host the inaugural SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum from 20‑22 November 2024 at Purana Qila, New Delhi. The event is jointly organised by the Ministry of Culture and the Ministry of External Affairs to deepen cultural‑strategic linkages among SCO member states.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Bilateral, regional and global groupings involving India (Shanghai Cooperation Organisation); International Relations – Cultural diplomacy and soft power.

Key Data & Agreements: SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum – 1st edition

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The forum expands the SCO’s agenda beyond security and economics to include civilisational and cultural cooperation, signalling a shift towards a more holistic regional bloc that can counterbalance Western‑led cultural narratives.
  • Impact on India: Enhances India’s soft‑power outreach within Central Asia and Eurasia, provides a platform to showcase Indian heritage, and creates avenues for people‑to‑people contact that can translate into greater economic and strategic cooperation with SCO members such as Russia, China, Kazakhstan and Iran.
  • Way Forward: India should institutionalise the civilisational dialogue as an annual event, link it with trade and connectivity initiatives (e.g., BIMSTEC, BBIN), and leverage cultural exchanges to build consensus on security and multilateral issues within the SCO framework.

Prelims Pointers

  • First SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum – 20‑22 Nov 2024, Purana Qila, New Delhi.
  • Organisers: Ministry of Culture & Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India.
  • SCO members include China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, India, Pakistan, Iran (observer).
  • Strategic intent: broaden SCO’s scope to cultural diplomacy, reinforcing India’s soft‑power in the region.
