‘I was in awe of Indira Gandhi; she was formidable,’ says Sonia Gandhi at launch of her memoir Belonging

16 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: On 16 Nov 2023, Sonia Gandhi launched her memoir “Belonging” in New Delhi. The event was attended by senior leaders of the INDIA alliance, including Akhilesh Yadav (Samajwadi Party), Abhishek Banerjee (TMC), Tamil Nadu CM C. Joseph Vijay, and former Punjab CM Amarinder Singh, underscoring the cross‑regional political significance of the Congress leader’s personal narrative.

Key Data & Stats: 16 Nov 2023 (date of launch) | INDIA bloc (multi‑party opposition alliance) | “Belonging” (title of memoir)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Sonia Gandhi, former President of the Indian National Congress and Chairperson of the UPA, authored “Belonging” to chronicle her political journey and personal reflections. The launch coincides with the formation of the INDIA alliance, a coalition of opposition parties seeking a coordinated challenge to the incumbent government.
  • Significance: The presence of leaders from diverse regional parties signals an attempt to project a united front and to leverage Gandhi’s legacy for broader electoral mobilisation. It also highlights the continuing relevance of dynastic politics in shaping party narratives and voter perception.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: While memoirs can humanise political figures, they may also deepen partisan polarisation and distract from policy‑driven discourse. The event raises questions about the role of personal charisma versus institutional strength in party building, especially for a party facing organisational decay.
  • Way Forward: Strengthen internal democratic mechanisms within the Congress and allied parties (e.g., regular intra‑party elections, transparent candidate selection) as recommended by past Sarkaria and ARC reports on party reforms. Encourage issue‑based coalition building rather than reliance on individual legacies to enhance the credibility of the opposition.
