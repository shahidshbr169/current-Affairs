KPRS to stage protest on Nov. 20

16 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The Kisan and Progressive Rural Society (KPRS) has announced a protest on Nov 20 demanding that the government withdraw the newly imposed ceiling on crop‑insurance payouts, which limits compensation to only 40% of the actual loss suffered by farmers.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (implementation of agricultural schemes, statutory bodies) & GS Paper III – Economy (agricultural finance, insurance).

Key Data & Stats: 40% compensation limit, Nov 20 protest date

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) provides subsidised crop‑insurance to mitigate losses from natural calamities. Recent policy circulars have capped insurer payouts at 40% of declared loss, prompting farmer groups to claim that the revision undermines the scheme’s original intent.
  • Significance: Reduced compensation directly affects farm‑household incomes, jeopardises food‑security objectives, and may erode confidence in central agricultural interventions, thereby influencing rural‑political stability.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: • Low compensation creates a moral hazard for insurers and disincentivises claim filing.
    • Lack of transparent grievance redressal mechanisms.
    • Potential conflict with the constitutional directive under Article 46 (promotion of educational and economic interests of weaker sections).
    • Over‑reliance on a single central scheme without adequate state‑level coordination.
  • Way Forward: • Constitute a high‑level review committee (e.g., under the Agricultural Reforms Commission) to assess the impact of the 40% cap.
    • Recommend raising the payout ceiling to at least 80% of actual loss, aligned with international best practices.
    • Strengthen the role of State Agricultural Universities and the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority (IRDA) in claim verification.
    • Institutionalise a farmer‑friendly grievance redressal cell under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare.

Prelims Pointers

  • Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) – central crop‑insurance scheme.
  • Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority (IRDA) – statutory body overseeing insurance implementation.
  • Article 46 of the Indian Constitution – state’s duty to promote the welfare of weaker sections.
  • Current compensation ceiling: 40% of actual loss.
