RRS decries failure to implement Sri Bagh Pact

16 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The Rayalaseema Rashtra Samithi (RRS) president criticised the state government's failure to honour the commitments made under the historic Sri Bagh Pact (1990) for the development of the Rayalaseema region, urging citizens to transcend party politics and launch a grassroots movement for regional progress.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (regional development, inter‑state agreements, implementation of policy promises)

Key Data & Stats: Rayalaseema Rashtra Samithi (RRS) Rayalaseema region – 5 districts

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The Sri Bagh Pact (1990) was a political accord between major parties promising a focused development package for Rayalaseema, a historically under‑developed segment of Andhra Pradesh. Over the decades, the promised infrastructure, irrigation and industrial projects have seen limited progress, leading to recurring agitations.
  • Significance: The grievance highlights the challenges of implementing region‑specific promises within a federal structure, raises questions on accountability of state governments, and underscores the need for equitable development to prevent regional alienation.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis:
    • Absence of a dedicated implementation agency and clear monitoring mechanism.
    • Fiscal constraints and competing priorities at the state level.
    • Potential political misuse of regional demands for electoral gains.
    • Legal ambiguity – the pact is a political agreement, not a statutory instrument, limiting enforceability.
  • Way Forward:
    • Constitute a Rayalaseema Development Authority under the NITI Aayog with representation from the state, central ministries and civil society.
    • Adopt a phased implementation plan with specific targets (e.g., irrigation coverage 30 % increase in 5 years).
    • Periodic review by an independent committee (e.g., a sub‑committee of the Arunachal‑Rajasthan Committee (ARC) on regional disparities).
    • Link central grants to measurable outcomes to ensure fiscal commitment.

Prelims Pointers

  • Sri Bagh Pact – 1990 political agreement for Rayalaseema development.

  • Rayalaseema Rashtra Samithi (RRS) – Regional political outfit demanding implementation.
  • Rayalaseema – Consists of Anantapur, Kurnool, Kadapa, Chittoor, and Nellore districts.
