Nepal flash floods: Kathmandu begins process to declare 213 missing Indians presumed dead

26 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Severe flash floods in Kathmandu have left 213 Indians missing, presumed dead. The Indian Embassy in Nepal has opened a channel for objections and concerns while coordinating relief efforts.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Bilateral relations (India‑Nepal), regional groupings (SAARC, BIMSTEC), and disaster management cooperation.

Key Data & Agreements: 213 missing Indians | India‑Nepal MoU on Disaster Management (2005) | SAARC Emergency Relief Coordination Centre (ERCC)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The floods underscore the vulnerability of the Himalayan region to climate‑induced disasters, testing the efficacy of regional mechanisms like SAARC and BIMSTEC in coordinated humanitarian response.
