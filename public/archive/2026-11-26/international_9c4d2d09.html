FCCPC urges supermarkets to protect consumer trust

26 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Nigeria’s Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC) has called on supermarket and shopping‑mall operators nationwide to enhance consumer‑protection measures and build trust through greater transparency in their business practices.

Key Data & Agreements: WTO TBT Agreement, UNCTAD Consumer Protection Guidelines

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: Strengthening consumer‑protection regimes aligns Nigeria with global norms under the WTO’s Technical Barriers to Trade (TBT) Agreement and UNCTAD guidelines, fostering a predictable environment for cross‑border retail investments.
  • Impact on India: Indian retail chains (e.g., Reliance Retail, Future Group) and FMCG exporters operating in Nigeria will need to adapt to tighter transparency and grievance‑redress mechanisms, influencing market entry strategies and bilateral trade flows.
