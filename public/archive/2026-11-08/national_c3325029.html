Fresh sexual harassment complaint against ‘Gem Granites’ Veeramani

8 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: A fresh sexual harassment complaint has been lodged against the proprietor of Gem Granites, Veeramani, based on the statement of a minor who earlier acted as a witness in an already registered case. The development underscores heightened scrutiny of workplace harassment and the legal safeguards for minors in India.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; GS Paper III – Social Justice, Women and Child Development

Key Data & Stats: Minor witness Previously registered case

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH Act) mandates an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) for every employer. In parallel, the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 extends special protection to minors. Recent judicial pronouncements have reinforced employer liability for harassment involving minors.
  • Significance: The case highlights the intersection of two vulnerable groups – women and children – and tests the efficacy of statutory mechanisms (POSH, POCSO) in ensuring swift redressal. It also raises questions about corporate accountability and the role of regulatory oversight in small‑scale enterprises.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: • Delayed registration of complaints often hampers evidence collection.
    • Power asymmetry between a minor and a business owner can deter reporting.
    • Limited capacity of local law enforcement and the ICC to handle cases involving minors.
    • Over‑reliance on victim testimony without corroborative forensic evidence may affect prosecution.
  • Way Forward: • Strengthen fast‑track courts for POSH and POCSO cases.
    • Mandate periodic compliance audits of ICCs, especially in MSMEs.
    • Establish a dedicated Child‑Sensitive Redressal Cell within the Ministry of Women and Child Development.
    • Encourage the formation of a joint oversight committee (including members of the National Commission for Women and NCPCR) to monitor implementation of POSH in workplaces employing minors.

Prelims Pointers

  • POSH Act, 2013 – mandates Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) for every employer.
  • POCSO Act, 2012 – provides special protection and procedural safeguards for minors.
  • Section 354A IPC – criminalizes sexual harassment of women.
  • Supreme Court judgment in Vishaka vs. State of Haryana (1997) – laid down the Vishaka Guidelines, the precursor to the POSH Act.
