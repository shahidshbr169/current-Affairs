U.S. federal court strikes down fixed terms for student, exchange visas

17 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: A U.S. federal court has invalidated a Department of Homeland Security rule that would have capped the stay of international students and exchange visitors at four years without special federal approval. The ruling restores the previous flexible duration framework for student and exchange visas.
