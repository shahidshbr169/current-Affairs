Students must embrace AI, digital skills to improve employability: ANU V-C

17 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: At the College Summit 2026, ANU Vice‑Chancellor Gangadhara Rao urged students to acquire AI and digital competencies to boost employability. Eight AI‑driven websites/apps were demonstrated, with three earmarked for further enterprise development.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper III – Science & Technology, Skill Development & Employment; GS Paper II – Education, Governance & Policy (Skill‑development schemes, NEP‑2020, Digital India).

Key Data & Stats: College Summit 2026 8 AI‑based websites/apps showcased 3 projects selected for enterprise development

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The National Skill Development Mission (2015) and NEP‑2020 stress future‑ready curricula. Recent NITI Aayog reports highlight a projected AI‑skill gap of 2‑3 million jobs by 2027, prompting higher‑education institutions to embed AI modules.
  • Significance: Aligning academic output with industry demand enhances youth employability, reduces structural unemployment, and supports India’s ambition to become a global AI hub under the “AI for All” strategy.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: • Limited faculty expertise in AI; • Inadequate infrastructure in tier‑2/3 colleges; • Data‑privacy and ethical concerns around student‑generated AI solutions; • Need for regulatory oversight to ensure curriculum standards (UGC, AICTE).
  • Way Forward: • Form a joint task‑force of UGC, AICTE, NITI Aayog, and industry bodies (NASSCOM) to design a standardized AI‑skill framework. • Leverage existing schemes like PM‑KVY and Skill India to fund AI labs in colleges. • Periodic review by the Committee on Skill Development (akin to the Sarkaria Committee) to monitor implementation and address ethical safeguards.

Prelims Pointers

  • AI‑skill gap projection – 2‑3 million jobs (NITI Aayog, 2023).
  • National Education Policy 2020 – emphasis on digital literacy & AI integration.
  • Skill India Mission – target of 400 million skilling beneficiaries by 2025.
  • Digital India Programme – aims for 1 billion internet users, creating a base for AI adoption.
