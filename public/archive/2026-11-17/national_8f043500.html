Modi’s governance journey marked by coordination across govt departments, focus on citizen experience: P.K. Mishra

17 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Principal Secretary P.K. Mishra highlighted that the Modi administration’s governance trajectory is characterized by strong inter‑departmental coordination and a citizen‑centric approach, showcasing how decisive leadership can convert vision into concrete actions.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Public Administration, Policy Implementation; GS Paper III – Ethics in Public Administration

Key Data & Stats:

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The government has pursued integrated policy frameworks such as Digital India, PM‑Kisan, and the National Digital Health Mission, aiming to break silos across ministries and place citizen experience at the core of service delivery.
  • Significance: Enhanced coordination reduces duplication, accelerates decision‑making, and improves public‑service quality, thereby strengthening the social contract and fostering trust in institutions.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Institutional inertia, fragmented data ecosystems, and limited accountability mechanisms can impede seamless coordination; there is also a risk of top‑down directives bypassing local autonomy.
  • Way Forward: Institutionalise inter‑ministerial task forces with clear KPIs, adopt performance‑dashboard systems, and embed citizen‑feedback loops (e.g., through the National Feedback Mechanism). Drawing on ARC recommendations, promote capacity‑building for mid‑level officials and strengthen the role of the Department of Administrative Reforms (DAR) to monitor implementation.

  • Principal Secretary to the Prime Minister – role in policy coordination.
  • Citizen‑experience focus – aligns with Digital India and Service Delivery Reforms.
  • Inter‑departmental coordination mechanisms – e.g., Joint Secretary‑level committees.
