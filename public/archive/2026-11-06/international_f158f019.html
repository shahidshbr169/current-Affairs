India-born Nithya Raman beats Democratic incumbent to become LA mayor

6 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: India‑born activist Nithya Raman has been elected mayor of Los Angeles, becoming the first Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) representative to lead the city that will host the 2028 Olympics. Her victory underscores the growing political influence of the Indian diaspora in a major global metropolis.
