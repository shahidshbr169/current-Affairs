China's Macau jails ex-lawmaker in first national security trial

6 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Macau’s Special Administrative Region has sentenced former legislator Au Kam San, a veteran organiser of Tiananmen‑square vigils, in the first trial under the expanded national‑security legislation of 2023. The case marks the inaugural application of the law in Macau, signalling Beijing’s broader push to curb dissent across its SARs.
