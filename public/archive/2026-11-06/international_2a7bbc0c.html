Asian parties reject ‘unilateral actions’, call for respect for international law

Core Summary: At the 13th General Assembly of the International Conference of Asian Political Parties (ICAPP) in Seoul, 83 Asian political parties unanimously condemned unilateral actions and urged respect for international law.
