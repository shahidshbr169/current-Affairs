Asian parties reject ‘unilateral actions’, call for respect for international law

6 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: At the 13th General Assembly of the International Conference of Asian Political Parties (ICAPP) in Seoul (Nov 4‑6), 83 Asian political parties unanimously adopted a declaration condemning unilateral actions and urging respect for international law.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Regional and global groupings; International organisations and treaties; India’s role in multilateral forums.

Key Data & Agreements: 13th ICAPP General Assembly, Seoul, 4‑6 Nov 2023 | 83 political parties from across Asia | Unanimous declaration on respect for international law

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The joint stance reflects growing Asian consensus against unilateralism, signalling a push for a rules‑based order amid heightened great‑power competition.
  • Impact on India: Reinforces India’s diplomatic narrative of strategic autonomy and multilateralism; provides a platform to align with like‑minded Asian partners on issues such as the South China Sea, Indo‑Pacific security, and climate commitments.
  • Way Forward: India should leverage ICAPP to deepen political dialogue, propose joint mechanisms for dispute resolution, and champion initiatives that operationalise the declaration (e.g., collaborative monitoring of unilateral actions).

Prelims Pointers

  • ICAPP – a regional political party forum comprising parties from 30+ Asian countries.
  • Declaration emphasizes respect for international law, countering unilateral actions by any state.
  • Seoul hosted the 13th Assembly, marking the first time the summit was held in East Asia since its inception.
  • India’s participation aligns with its “Act East” and “Indo‑Pacific” strategies.
