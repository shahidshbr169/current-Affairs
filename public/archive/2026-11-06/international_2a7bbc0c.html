Asian parties reject ‘unilateral actions’, call for respect for international law

6 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: At the 13th General Assembly of the International Conference of Asian Political Parties (ICAPP) in Seoul, 83 Asian political parties unanimously condemned unilateral actions and urged respect for international law.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Regional and global groupings involving India; International relations and law.

Key Data & Agreements: 13th ICAPP GA, Seoul, 4‑6 Nov 2024

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The collective stance signals a push for a rule‑based order in Asia, countering unilateral moves by major powers and reinforcing multilateral diplomacy.
  • Impact on India: Aligns with India’s “Act East” and “Strategic Autonomy” policies; offers diplomatic leverage in regional forums and strengthens India’s image as a champion of international law.
  • Way Forward: India should deepen engagement with ICAPP, promote joint statements on maritime security, and use the platform to advocate for adherence to UNCLOS and other international norms.

Prelims Pointers

  • ICAPP – a regional political grouping of Asian parties, 83 members participated.
  • Declaration adopted unanimously condemning unilateral actions.
  • Venue: Seoul, South Korea; dates: 4‑6 November 2024.
  • Emphasis on respect for international law, relevant to India’s stance on maritime disputes and global governance.
