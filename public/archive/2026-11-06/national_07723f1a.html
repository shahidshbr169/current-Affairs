Voters in SIR states can now refill Enumeration Forms to get back in final list instead of Form 6

6 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The Election Commission of India (ECI) has issued fresh directions for voters in seven states – Delhi, Maharashtra, Haryana, Andhra Pradesh, Jharkhand, Telangana and Nagaland – allowing them to refill the Enumeration Form to re‑enter the final electoral roll, instead of having to file a new Form 6. This move eases the procedural burden on voters who missed the earlier deadline and aims to plug gaps in the electoral roll.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; Electoral reforms; Representation of People Act, 1951; Role & powers of the Election Commission (Art 324).

Key Data & Stats: Delhi, Maharashtra, Haryana, Andhra Pradesh, Jharkhand, Telangana, Nagaland Form 6 Enumeration Form

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Earlier, voters who failed to submit the Enumeration Form by the stipulated deadline were excluded from the final list and had to file Form 6, a lengthier process. Several states reported a surge in pending cases, prompting the ECI to relax the rule for the identified SIR (State‑wise Inclusion Review) states.
  • Significance: The amendment safeguards the fundamental right to vote (Art 326) by reducing disenfranchisement, improves the accuracy of the electoral roll, and enhances public confidence in the electoral process—critical for the legitimacy of elections.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis:
    • Administrative load on Booth‑level Officers to verify and re‑enter data.
    • Risk of duplicate entries or fraudulent claims if verification mechanisms are weak.
    • Legal ambiguity regarding the status of already‑submitted Form 6 cases.
  • Way Forward:
    • Adopt a phased digital verification using Aadhaar‑seeding and OCR to streamline re‑entry.
    • Periodic audit of the electoral roll by an independent committee (e.g., a sub‑committee of the Sarkaria Commission on electoral reforms).
    • Issue clear guidelines on the coexistence of Form 6 and Enumeration Form to avoid legal disputes.
    • Strengthen capacity building for Booth‑level Officers through e‑training modules.

Prelims Pointers

  • Election Commission of India (ECI) – constitutional body under Art 324.
  • Form 6 – application for inclusion in the electoral roll under the Representation of People Act, 1951.
  • Enumeration Form – supplementary form to update voter details; now permissible for refill in specified states.
  • Fundamental Right to Vote – Art 326; importance of maintaining an up‑to‑date electoral roll.
