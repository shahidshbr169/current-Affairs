Voters in SIR states can now refill Enumeration Forms to get back in final list instead of Form 6

6 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The Election Commission of India (ECI) has issued directions allowing voters in the seven Special Incremental Register (SIR) states – Delhi, Maharashtra, Haryana, Andhra Pradesh, Jharkhand, Telangana and Nagaland – to refill the Enumeration Form to be reinstated in the final electoral roll, eliminating the need to file a fresh Form 6. This move streamlines the re‑entry process for voters who were previously removed, thereby reducing disenfranchisement.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (Electoral reforms, Election Commission, Representation of People Act, 1951); GS Paper I – Indian Society & Social Justice (voter inclusion).

Key Data & Stats: 7 SIR states Form 6 Enumeration Form Article 324 Representation of People Act, 1951

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Periodic revision of electoral rolls under the Representation of People Act often leads to removal of names deemed “inactive”. In SIR states, affected voters were required to submit Form 6 to contest the removal, a process that many found cumbersome and led to inadvertent exclusion.
  • Significance: The new direction safeguards the fundamental right to vote (Article 326) by simplifying reinstatement, enhances the credibility of the electoral roll, and aligns with the ECI’s mandate to ensure free and fair elections.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: • Administrative load on Booth‑level officers to verify refilled Enumeration Forms.
    • Risk of duplicate entries or fraudulent claims if verification mechanisms are weak.
    • Need for clear timelines to prevent prolonged disenfranchisement.
  • Way Forward: • Deploy Aadhaar‑linked authentication for faster verification.
    • Institutionalise a periodic “clean‑up” committee under the ECI, similar to the Sarkaria Commission model, to audit SIR roll updates.
    • Amend the Representation of People Act to formalise the Enumeration‑Form route as a statutory right for all states, not just SIR.

Prelims Pointers

  • Election Commission of India (ECI) – constitutional body under Article 324.
  • Form 6 – legal document for contesting removal from electoral roll (RPA, 1951).
  • Enumeration Form – used for updating/adding entries in the electoral roll.
  • Special Incremental Register (SIR) states – Delhi, Maharashtra, Haryana, Andhra Pradesh, Jharkhand, Telangana, Nagaland.
