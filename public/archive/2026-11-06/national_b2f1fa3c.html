Abhijeet Dipke & Prakash Raj appeal Kerala to join protest against SIR, EC on Nov 7

6 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Activist Abhijeet Dipke of the Cockroach Janta Party and actor Prakash Raj have called on students, youth and civil‑society groups in Kerala to rally against the Election Commission’s Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls. The protest is slated for 7 Nov 2026 in Thiruvananthapuram, highlighting concerns over the transparency and fairness of the roll‑revision process.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; Institutions of the State (Election Commission); Electoral reforms and public participation.

Key Data & Stats: SIR – Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls EC – Election Commission of India (Article 324) Kerala – 14 million registered voters (approx.)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The EC periodically undertakes Special Intensive Revision to purge duplicate, ineligible or deceased entries from electoral rolls. Recent SIR drives in several states have sparked apprehensions about potential disenfranchisement, especially among migrant and youth voters.
  • Significance: Electoral rolls are the backbone of free and fair elections. Any perceived arbitrariness in their revision can erode public confidence, affect voter turnout, and invite litigation, thereby impacting the legitimacy of the democratic process.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Lack of transparent criteria for deletion, inadequate grievance redressal mechanisms, and limited public awareness create scope for alleged bias. The EC’s quasi‑judicial status under Article 324 limits judicial intervention until after the final roll is published.
  • Way Forward: • Strengthen the EC’s grievance cell with a fast‑track online portal (modelled on the “Voter Helpline”).
    • Constitute an independent review committee (similar to the Sarkaria Commission’s approach) to audit SIR methodology.
    • Enhance civic education on roll‑verification processes through State Election Commissions and NGOs.
    • Periodic parliamentary oversight through a standing committee on elections to ensure procedural fairness.

Prelims Pointers

  • Election Commission of India – constitutional body under Article 324.
  • Special Intensive Revision (SIR) – periodic audit of electoral rolls to remove ineligible entries.
  • Right to vote – fundamental right under Article 326; any exclusion must follow due process.
  • Role of civil society – can invoke Article 19(1)(a) (freedom of speech) to protest and demand transparency.
