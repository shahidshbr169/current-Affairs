Sabarimala pilgrimage: KSRTC to operate special bus services to Pampa from airports

6 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Kerala State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC) will run special bus services linking the state’s airports to Pampa, the gateway for Sabarimala pilgrims. The move is aimed at easing travel and crowd management during the 2026‑27 Mandala‑Makaravilakku festival season.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; GS Paper III – Infrastructure, Transport, Urban Development; GS Paper I – Geography (pilgrimage tourism).

Key Data & Stats: 2026‑27 (festival season), Mandala‑Makaravilakku (annual pilgrimage period).

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Sabarimala attracts 5‑6 million devotees annually, with the Mandala‑Makaravilakku period witnessing the highest footfall. Historically, pilgrims faced bottlenecks in reaching Pampa due to limited public transport and reliance on private operators.
  • Significance: Enhanced airport‑to‑Pampa connectivity reduces travel time, curtails traffic congestion on state highways, improves safety, and boosts the local economy through increased pilgrim spend.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Coordination between KSRTC, airport authorities, and local police; funding for additional fleet; ensuring last‑mile connectivity from Pampa to the shrine; environmental impact on the ecologically sensitive Western Ghats.
  • Way Forward: • Form an inter‑departmental task‑force (Transport, Tourism, Forest) under the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways.
    • Adopt a Public‑Private Partnership model for fleet expansion and smart ticketing.
    • Periodic review by the ARC on Transport to assess capacity and suggest infrastructure upgrades (e.g., dedicated pilgrim corridors).
    • Integrate real‑time tracking and GIS‑based crowd‑management systems.

Prelims Pointers

  • Sabarimala pilgrimage – one of the world’s largest annual religious gatherings.
  • Mandala‑Makaravilakku festival – 41‑day period culminating in the Makaravilakku event.
  • KSRTC – State‑run transport undertaking responsible for inter‑city and intra‑state services.
  • Article 243 (1) – Role of State Governments in providing transport facilities under local self‑government.
