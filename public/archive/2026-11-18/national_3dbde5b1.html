Pinarayi flags healthcare crisis in Attappady, seeks ₹5 lakh for each infant death

18 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Kerala Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan highlighted a severe healthcare crisis in the tribal belt of Attappady, demanding a compensation of ₹5 lakh for every infant death. He warned that existing policies are aggravating the hardship of tribal communities and called for strong protests to compel corrective action.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (Public health administration, Tribal welfare, Article 21); GS Paper III – Social Justice (Tribal issues, Health & Nutrition); GS Paper I – Indian Geography (Kerala’s Attappady region).

Key Data & Stats: ₹5 lakh compensation per infant death | Attappady – tribal‑dominant sub‑district of Palakkad, Kerala

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Attappady, part of the Western Ghats, has historically lagged in health infrastructure. High infant mortality, malnutrition and limited access to tertiary care persist despite Kerala’s overall strong health indicators. Tribal Sub‑Plan (TSP) funds and National Health Mission schemes have been inadequately implemented.
  • Significance: The demand underscores the constitutional guarantee of the right to health under Article 21 and the state’s duty to protect vulnerable sections (Article 46). It also raises questions on the efficacy of existing tribal welfare mechanisms and the accountability of state health administration.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis:
    • Inadequate primary health centres (PHCs) and shortage of qualified medical staff in remote villages.
    • Fragmented data on infant mortality hampers targeted interventions.
    • Policy‑implementation gap: TSP allocations often remain unspent or are diverted.
    • Legal/ethical dilemma of monetary compensation versus systemic health improvements.
  • Way Forward:
    • Set up a dedicated Tribal Health Task Force under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (refer to the earlier ARC on Tribal Health, 2019).
    • Accelerate deployment of mobile health units and tele‑medicine facilities in Attappady.
    • Ensure full utilisation of TSP funds with periodic audit by the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG).
    • Adopt a rights‑based approach: enact a State‑level “Tribal Infant Health Act” aligning with Article 21.
    • Strengthen community participation through Panchayati Raj Institutions and tribal councils.

Prelims Pointers

  • Article 21 – Right to Life includes the right to health.
  • Tribal Sub‑Plan (TSP) – central & state scheme for tribal welfare.
  • National Health Mission (NHM) – flagship programme for rural health infrastructure.
  • Kerala’s Health Indicators – high overall performance but pockets like Attappady lag.
