DRDO conducts successful flight-test of Pralay missile

Core Summary: The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) successfully completed a flight‑test of the indigenously developed Pralay short‑range ballistic missile. The test, conducted as part of user‑evaluation trials, met all stipulated objectives as confirmed by data from range instrumentation.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper III – Defence & Security; Science & Technology; also relevant to GS Paper II – Governance (Ministry of Defence, DRDO as statutory body).

Key Data & Stats: No quantitative figures disclosed in the release.

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: The Pralay missile is a product of India’s Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) aimed at achieving strategic autonomy in short‑range ballistic capabilities. Development accelerated after the 2019 “Make in India” push for indigenous defence hardware.
  • Significance: A successful user‑evaluation trial enhances the Army’s operational readiness, strengthens deterrence against regional threats, and reduces dependence on imports, aligning with the “Atmanirbhar Bharat” defence policy.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Continued funding, technology transfer restrictions under the Missile Technology Control Regime (MTCR), and the need for seamless integration with existing command‑and‑control systems pose potential bottlenecks.
  • Way Forward: • Strengthen DRDO‑Army coordination through a joint steering committee.
    • Periodic review by the Defence Acquisition Council (DAC) for indigenisation targets.
    • Explore export potential under the “Strategic Export” framework after compliance with MTCR.
    • Consider a parliamentary oversight committee (similar to the ARC) to monitor timelines and cost‑effectiveness.

Prelims Pointers

  • Pralay – indigenous short‑range ballistic missile (SRBM) developed by DRDO.
  • User Evaluation Trials – final phase before induction into service.
  • Defence Minister Rajnath Singh announced the successful test.
  • Range instrumentation data validates performance parameters.
