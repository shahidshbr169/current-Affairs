Bangladesh vows to arrest ex-PM Hasina if she returns from India

23 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Bangladesh has warned that former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina will be arrested if she returns from India, urging New Delhi to honour the bilateral extradition treaty. The statement by State Minister Shama Obaed Islam underscores rising political tension and street unrest in Dhaka ahead of her planned December arrival.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Bilateral, regional and global groupings involving India; India‑Bangladesh relations; International treaties and extradition mechanisms.

Key Data & Agreements: India‑Bangladesh Extradition Treaty (signed 2001) • Planned return: December 2024 • Location of diplomatic note: Dhaka, Bangladesh

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The demand highlights the fragility of South Asian diplomatic norms on political asylum and extradition, testing the resilience of bilateral mechanisms amid domestic upheavals in Bangladesh.
  • Impact on India: India faces a diplomatic balancing act—upholding treaty obligations while avoiding perception of interference in Bangladesh’s internal politics, which could affect strategic cooperation on security, water sharing, and connectivity projects.
  • Way Forward: India should engage in high‑level diplomatic dialogue, clarify the legal basis of the extradition request, and, if needed, seek a mutually acceptable judicial review mechanism to preserve bilateral trust and regional stability.

Prelims Pointers

  • India‑Bangladesh Extradition Treaty (2001) – legal framework for surrender of fugitives.
  • Bangladesh’s political volatility ahead of national elections; street violence reported.
  • Strategic significance of India‑Bangladesh ties in SAARC, BIMSTEC, and regional security architecture.
  • Potential implications for India’s “Neighborhood First” policy and cross‑border water/resource negotiations.
