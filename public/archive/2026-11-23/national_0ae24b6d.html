Veena Sikri assesses Bangladesh's political crisis amid Sheikh Hasina's planned return

23 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Veena Sikri analysed the escalating political crisis in Bangladesh as Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina prepares to return to Dhaka. The assessment highlighted the country’s economic slowdown, surging inflation and civil unrest, while reiterating India’s pledge to ensure her safe arrival.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; International Relations – India‑Bangladesh bilateral ties; GS Paper III – Security, Law & Order.

Key Data & Stats: Bangladesh political crisis Economic slowdown Rising inflation Sheikh Hasina’s planned return

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Bangladesh has been grappling with a volatile post‑election environment, marked by opposition protests, a depreciating currency and a slowdown in GDP growth. The political vacuum intensified after the abrupt resignation of former Prime Minister Khaleda Zia’s party leaders, creating uncertainty over governance.
  • Significance: Stability in Bangladesh is crucial for India’s northeastern security, trade corridors (e.g., BBIN), and the broader South‑Asian geopolitical balance. Economic distress can spill over as cross‑border migration, illicit trade and security challenges.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Ensuring Hasina’s safe return raises questions about sovereignty, diplomatic protocol and the role of external actors. Domestically, Bangladesh’s high inflation (inflation pressure) strains public services, while the government’s crackdown on dissent may attract international scrutiny.
  • Way Forward: • Strengthen bilateral mechanisms (Joint Working Group on Security & Economic Cooperation).
    • Encourage confidence‑building measures through SAARC/ BIMSTEC platforms.
    • India could offer technical assistance for fiscal stabilization (e.g., IMF‑style programme support).
    • Periodic review by a joint India‑Bangladesh committee (akin to an ARC‑type body) to monitor political developments and mitigate security risks.

Prelims Pointers

  • Bangladesh political crisis – impact on regional stability.
  • Sheikh Hasina’s planned return – diplomatic protocol & security arrangements.
  • Economic slowdown & rising inflation in Bangladesh – implications for trade and migration.
  • India’s commitment to ensure safe return – reflects India’s strategic interest in its eastern neighbourhood.
