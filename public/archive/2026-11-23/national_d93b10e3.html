Former High Commissioner Veena Sikri discusses political unrest and Sheikh Hasina's return plans

23 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Former High Commissioner Veena Sikri warned that Bangladesh could face heightened political unrest if Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina returns in 2024 amid worsening economic indicators and rising unemployment. The commentary underscores the potential spill‑over effects on India‑Bangladesh bilateral ties and regional stability.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; International Relations (GS Paper III) – India’s foreign policy & regional cooperation.

Key Data & Stats: 2024 (year of anticipated return); economic slowdown & rising unemployment in Bangladesh (no specific figures provided).

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Bangladesh’s Awami League has ruled since 2009. Recent macro‑economic stress—declining GDP growth, trade deficits, and a surge in joblessness—has fueled public discontent. Sheikh Hasina’s scheduled return after a foreign visit is being viewed as a flashpoint for protests.
  • Significance: Political volatility in Bangladesh can affect India’s border management, cross‑border trade (≈ $5 billion annually), and security cooperation on issues such as terrorism, migration, and river‑water sharing. It also tests India’s diplomatic balancing act between strategic partnership and democratic values.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: • Risk of democratic backsliding and suppression of dissent. • Potential rise in anti‑India sentiment if unrest escalates. • Limited leverage for India without overt interference, given the principle of non‑intervention under the UN Charter.
  • Way Forward: • Institutionalise a high‑level India‑Bangladesh dialogue mechanism (e.g., via BIMSTEC) to monitor political‑economic developments. • Deploy a joint economic task‑force to address unemployment and trade deficits. • Encourage civil‑society exchanges and people‑to‑people contact to build resilience against misinformation. • Consider a periodic review by the Ministry of External Affairs in consultation with the National Security Council.

Prelims Pointers

  • Sheikh Hasina – Prime Minister of Bangladesh, leader of the Awami League.
  • Political unrest linked to economic slowdown and rising unemployment in Bangladesh.
  • India‑Bangladesh strategic partnership: trade, water‑sharing (Treaty of 1996), and security cooperation.
  • Role of the High Commissioner as a diplomatic conduit for managing bilateral concerns.
