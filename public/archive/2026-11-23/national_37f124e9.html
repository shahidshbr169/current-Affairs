UCBs should support sustainable growth through prudent lending: RBI Guv

23 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: RBI Governor Shaktikanta Das has urged Urban Co‑operatives Banks (UCBs) to align their credit‑disbursement with sustainable growth by practising prudent lending, strengthening governance, mitigating cyber‑risk, enhancing customer‑centricity and undertaking Mission SAKSHAM training.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper III – Indian Economy (Banking sector, financial inclusion, credit delivery); GS Paper II – Governance (Regulatory bodies, RBI, corporate governance of banks)

Key Data & Stats: Mission SAKSHAM

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: UCBs constitute a significant part of the credit ecosystem for micro‑enterprises and rural‑urban fringe areas. Past episodes of high NPA levels, weak governance and limited digital infrastructure have prompted the RBI to tighten supervision and promote capacity‑building measures.
  • Significance: Sustainable credit flow from UCBs can boost MSME growth, employment generation and inclusive development while safeguarding financial stability. Emphasis on cyber‑risk and customer‑centricity aligns with the broader digital‑finance agenda of the government.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Many UCBs suffer from inadequate capital base, outdated IT systems, and governance lapses. Implementing robust cyber‑security frameworks and training programmes like Mission SAKSHAM requires resources and coordinated oversight.
  • Way Forward: • RBI should operationalise a tiered supervisory framework with periodic stress‑testing of UCBs.
    • Encourage adoption of Basel‑III norms proportionate to UCB size.
    • Set up a joint task‑force (RBI + Ministry of Finance + NABARD) to monitor cyber‑risk compliance.
    • Expand Mission SAKSHAM modules to cover digital literacy, ESG‑linked lending and risk‑based pricing.
    • Periodic review by the Committee on Financial Sector Reforms (ARC‑type) to assess impact on sustainable growth.

Prelims Pointers

  • RBI – Central banking authority and regulator of UCBs.
  • Urban Co‑operatives Banks (UCBs) – Statutory banks under the Multi‑State Co‑operative Societies Act.
  • Mission SAKSHAM – RBI‑initiated capacity‑building programme for UCB staff.
  • Prudent lending, governance, cyber‑risk safeguards, customer centricity – Key focus areas for sustainable credit growth.
