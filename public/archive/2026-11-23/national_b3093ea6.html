All permissions for A.P. projects in Krishna basin were given during KCR regime: Revanth Reddy

23 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Opposition leader Revanth Reddy alleged that every clearance for Andhra Pradesh’s water‑related projects in the Krishna basin was granted during the tenure of Telangana Chief Minister K. Chandrashekar Rao (KCR). The claim spotlights inter‑state water‑allocation politics post‑bifurcation and raises questions on procedural propriety.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity (Inter‑State River Water Disputes, Centre‑State relations); GS Paper III – Environment & Ecology (Water resources management)

Key Data & Stats: Krishna Basin – covers parts of Telangana & Andhra Pradesh KCR regime – 2014 to present Inter‑State River Water Disputes Act, 1956 (as per Article 262)

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: After the 2014 bifurcation of Andhra Pradesh, the Krishna River became a contested resource. The Krishna Water Disputes Tribunal (KWDT) award (2016) allocated 80 % of Krishna water to AP and 20 % to Telangana, subject to periodic reviews. Project clearances (e.g., lift‑irrigation schemes, reservoirs) require concurrence from the Central Water Commission (CWC) and, where inter‑state impact exists, the Ministry of Jal Shakti.
  • Significance: Water is critical for agriculture, drinking supply, and industrial growth in both states. Perceived bias in granting permissions can fuel political friction, affect farmer livelihoods, and impede cooperative basin‑wide planning.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis:
    • Legal ambiguity: Article 262 places river‑related disputes under central jurisdiction, yet state‑level clearances often proceed without a final tribunal order.
    • Administrative overlap: CWC, State Water Resources Departments, and the Ministry of Jal Shakti may duplicate approvals, creating loopholes for political influence.
    • Data deficit: Lack of transparent, real‑time water‑availability data hampers objective decision‑making.
  • Way Forward:
    • Constitute a joint Inter‑State Water Management Committee (as recommended by the ARC) with equal representation from AP, Telangana, and the Centre to vet all basin‑wide projects.
    • Mandate that any new project undergo a pre‑clearance review by the Krishna River Basin Authority (KRBA) under the Ministry of Jal Shakti, ensuring compliance with the KWDT award.
    • Adopt a transparent GIS‑based water‑resource dashboard accessible to the public, reducing speculation and politicisation.
    • Consider referral of any pending disputes to the Supreme Court under Article 131 for a definitive adjudication.

Prelims Pointers

  • Inter‑State River Water Disputes Act, 1956 – empowers Centre to adjudicate river disputes.
  • Article 262 of the Constitution – places river‑water disputes under Parliament’s exclusive jurisdiction.
  • Krishna Water Disputes Tribunal (KWDT) award (2016) – allocation percentages between AP & Telangana.
  • Central Water Commission (CWC) – apex technical body for water‑resource project approvals.
