Pakistan PM Shehbaz Sharif to undertake official visit to Russia

23 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: Pakistan’s Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif will embark on a three‑day official visit to Russia starting Nov 30, 2024. The trip aims to deepen bilateral ties by finalising energy‑supply and investment agreements.

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The visit signals Russia’s outreach to South Asia amid Western sanctions, and Pakistan’s attempt to diversify energy sources and attract Russian capital, potentially reshaping regional power equations.
  • Impact on India: Strengthened Pak‑Russia ties could affect India’s strategic calculus on energy security, may create competition for Russian investment in the subcontinent, and could influence the dynamics of the SCO and Indo‑Pak dialogue.
