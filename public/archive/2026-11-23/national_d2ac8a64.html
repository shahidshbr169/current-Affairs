India-U.S. defence ties given momentum by growing number of military exercises

23 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The United States has labelled its defence relationship with India as a “Major Defence Partnership” and highlighted that four large‑scale joint military exercises across land, air and maritime domains were conducted in 2026. The expanding exercise calendar signals deeper operational interoperability and strategic convergence between the two democracies.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper III – Defence, Security and International Relations; GS Paper IV – India’s Foreign Policy and Bilateral Relations

Key Data & Stats: “Major Defence Partnership” (U.S. Embassy fact sheet), Four major joint exercises in 2026, Land, Air & Maritime domains

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Defence cooperation accelerated after the 2020 “2+2” dialogue, the signing of the Logistics Exchange Memorandum of Agreement (LEMOA) and the Basic Exchange and Cooperation Agreement (BECA). Regular exercises such as Yudh Abhyas, Malabar, and Cope India have built confidence and interoperability.
  • Significance: Enhances India’s strategic autonomy in the Indo‑Pacific, improves joint operational readiness, facilitates technology transfer, and sends a calibrated signal to regional actors, especially China, about a united front on maritime security and freedom of navigation.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: Procurement bottlenecks under the Defence Procurement Procedure, clearance delays for foreign equipment, concerns over sovereignty in sharing sensitive data under BECA, and the need to balance relations with other major powers.
  • Way Forward: • Institutionalise a Joint Defence Steering Committee under the Ministry of Defence and the U.S. Department of Defense.
    • Fast‑track approvals for joint R&D projects under the “Strategic Partnership” framework.
    • Periodic review by an expert panel (e.g., a sub‑committee of the ARC) to assess operational outcomes and legal safeguards.
    • Expand multilateral exercises under the Quad to embed India‑U.S. coordination within broader regional architecture.

Prelims Pointers

  • “Major Defence Partnership” – terminology used by the U.S. Embassy.
  • Four joint exercises in 2026 covering land, air and maritime domains.
  • Key agreements underpinning the partnership: LEMOA (Logistics Exchange), BECA (Basic Exchange and Cooperation Agreement).
  • Strategic grouping relevance: Quad (U.S., India, Japan, Australia) and Indo‑Pacific security architecture.
