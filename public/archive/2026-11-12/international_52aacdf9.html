G7 Foreign Ministers say China's actions east of Taiwan threaten stability

12 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: The G7 foreign ministers issued a joint statement warning that China's military activities east of Taiwan pose a threat to regional peace and stability. They reaffirmed the need to maintain calm across the Taiwan Strait.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Bilateral, regional and global groupings involving India; International Relations – India’s strategic autonomy and security architecture.

Key Data & Agreements: 2024 G7 Foreign Ministers’ Statement

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The warning underscores growing great‑power competition in the Indo‑Pacific, where China’s assertiveness challenges the existing security order and tests the cohesion of Western alliances.
  • Impact on India: While India is not a G7 member, the statement aligns with New Delhi’s concerns over freedom of navigation, the “One China” policy, and the risk of escalation that could affect Indian trade routes and maritime interests.
  • Way Forward: India can deepen engagement with like‑minded groupings (Quad, IPEF), bolster its own naval presence in the Indian Ocean, and continue diplomatic balancing by urging restraint while upholding strategic autonomy.

Prelims Pointers

  • G7 comprises the US, UK, Canada, France, Germany, Italy and Japan.
  • China’s actions east of Taiwan include naval and air deployments that raise the risk of inadvertent conflict.
  • India’s “strategic autonomy” policy emphasizes non‑alignment while cooperating with Quad members on Indo‑Pacific security.
  • The Taiwan Strait is a critical maritime corridor for global trade, including Indian energy imports.
