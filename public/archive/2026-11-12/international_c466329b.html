Nvidia preparing to oust CEO Jensen Huang: U.S. media

12 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: U.S. media reports indicate that Nvidia is preparing to remove its co‑founder‑CEO Jensen Huang following revelations of personal misconduct. The leadership shake‑up comes at a time when Nvidia’s AI chips are pivotal to global technology supply chains and the U.S.–China strategic rivalry.

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: Nvidia’s dominance in AI accelerators makes it a strategic asset in the U.S. effort to maintain technological superiority over China. A leadership crisis could affect supply‑chain reliability, influencing the pace of AI adoption worldwide and potentially prompting policy shifts in export controls.

  • Way Forward: • Strengthen domestic semiconductor ecosystem under the PLI (Production‑Linked Incentive) Scheme to reduce over‑reliance on a single foreign supplier.
    • Engage with U.S. authorities to ensure continuity of critical AI hardware under existing export‑control licences.
