India, Canada eye defence pact during Modi visit in December

19 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: During Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s December visit to Canada, both governments signalled a mutual intent to negotiate a defence cooperation pact. India’s High Commissioner to Ottawa cautioned that peripheral issues must not derail the broader strategic partnership.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Bilateral, regional and global groupings involving India; India‑Canada strategic relations; defence and security cooperation.

Key Data & Agreements: Proposed Defence Cooperation Agreement | December (year unspecified) visit | 2018 India‑Canada Strategic Partnership Framework

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The pact would deepen Indo‑Canadian alignment in the Indo‑Pacific, diversify India’s defence procurement away from traditional partners, and reinforce a liberal democratic bloc countering China’s growing influence.
  • Impact on India: Access to Canadian aerospace and cyber‑defence technologies, joint R&D opportunities, and enhanced interoperability through joint exercises; potential boost to the ‘Make in India’ defence sector via technology transfer.
  • Way Forward: Formalise a MoU outlining joint research, co‑production and training; set up a bilateral Defence Dialogue mechanism; address trade‑related frictions (e.g., agricultural disputes) to ensure a conducive environment for security cooperation.

Prelims Pointers

  • India and Canada signed the 2018 Strategic Partnership Framework covering defence, energy and education.
  • Canada ranks among the top five global arms exporters, offering advanced aerospace and cyber‑defence capabilities.
  • The proposed pact aligns with India’s ‘Act East’ and ‘Indo‑Pacific’ strategic doctrines.
  • Both nations are members of the Quad (U.S., Japan, Australia) and share common concerns over maritime security in the Indo‑Pacific.
