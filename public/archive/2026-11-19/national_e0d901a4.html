CWRDM officials inspect Wayanad township to address waterlogging

19 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: Officials of the Centre for Water Resources Development and Management (CWRDM) inspected the Wayanad township to assess chronic water‑logging. They have been directed to submit a detailed remedial report next month outlining measures to prevent rainwater ingress into houses and to improve drainage.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Decentralisation & Local Administration; GS Paper III – Environment & Ecology (Urban Water Management)

Key Data & Stats: Wayanad district, Kerala | CWRDM (Centre for Water Resources Development and Management) | Report due next month

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Recurrent monsoon‑induced water‑logging in hilly terrains of Wayanad has exposed gaps in storm‑water infrastructure, prompting state agencies to seek technical expertise from CWRDM.
  • Significance: Persistent water‑logging hampers public health, disrupts livelihoods, and undermines the objectives of the National Water Policy (2012) and Swachh Bharat Mission’s focus on sanitation and safe water.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis: • Fragmented jurisdiction between Panchayats, Municipalities and the Public Works Department.
    • Inadequate capacity of existing drainage networks to handle high‑intensity rainfall.
    • Lack of an integrated storm‑water management plan and insufficient community participation.
  • Way Forward: • Constitute a multi‑stakeholder task force under the State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA) to oversee implementation.
    • Adopt Integrated Water Resources Management (IWRM) principles – rain‑water harvesting, retention basins, and regular desilting of drains.
    • Leverage guidelines of the National Urban Transport Policy (2020) for “green” drainage corridors.
    • Periodic monitoring by CWRDM and capacity‑building of local bodies through the Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs’ “Smart Cities” framework.

Prelims Pointers

  • Centre for Water Resources Development and Management (CWRDM) – technical agency for water‑resource issues.
  • Wayanad district – prone to monsoon‑related water‑logging; relevance to State Disaster Management Authority.
  • National Water Policy, 2012 – emphasizes rain‑water harvesting and integrated drainage.
  • Integrated Water Resources Management (IWRM) – recommended approach for urban flood mitigation.
