A total of 28 illegal immigrants apprehended, expelled: Assam CM Himanta Biswa Sarma

7 November 2026•2 min read

Core Summary: The Assam government reported that 28 illegal immigrants were apprehended and subsequently expelled. The Chief Minister reiterated that the state will persist with its anti‑infiltration drive, emphasizing that Assam’s security and ethnic identity will not be compromised.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Governance, Constitution, Polity; GS Paper III – Internal Security, Law & Order

Key Data & Stats: 28 persons expelled; ongoing drive post-1985 Assam Accord; reference to 2019 NRC final list; governed under Foreigners Act, 1946

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Background: Assam has a long‑standing issue of cross‑border infiltration from Bangladesh, intensified after the 1985 Assam Accord which promised to identify and deport illegal migrants. The National Register of Citizens (NRC) exercise in 2019 aimed to update the citizen list, leading to heightened vigilance by state agencies.
  • Significance: The apprehension underscores the state’s commitment to safeguarding demographic balance, protecting the cultural‑linguistic identity enshrined in the Accord, and maintaining internal security. It also signals political will to enforce the Foreigners Act and related state‑level anti‑infiltration policies.
  • Challenges / Critical Analysis:
    • Ensuring due‑process rights of detainees while expediting deportation.
    • Potential humanitarian concerns and diplomatic friction with Bangladesh.
    • Resource constraints for continuous border monitoring across a 1,700‑km frontier.
    • Risk of communal polarization if enforcement is perceived as selective.
  • Way Forward:
    • Strengthen border fencing and install biometric surveillance at entry points (recommendation of the Border Management Committee).
    • Form a joint Assam‑Bangladesh task force under the Ministry of Home Affairs for coordinated patrolling.
    • Periodic review by an independent committee (e.g., a re‑constituted Sarkaria‑type commission) to assess the impact on human rights and suggest corrective measures.
    • Utilize technology‑driven verification (Aadhaar linkage, facial recognition) to streamline identification under the Foreigners Act.

Prelims Pointers

  • Assam Accord (1985) – agreement to detect and deport illegal migrants.
  • National Register of Citizens (NRC) – 2019 final list for Assam.
  • Foreigners Act, 1946 – legal framework for detection and deportation of illegal immigrants.
  • Infiltration – term used for illegal cross‑border migration, a key security concern for the Northeast.
