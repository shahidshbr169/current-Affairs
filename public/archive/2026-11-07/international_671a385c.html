Top U.S., China officials raise trade 'concerns' as APEC approaches

7 November 2026•1 min read

Core Summary: U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer held a bilateral call with Chinese Vice‑Premier He Lifeng to raise mutual trade concerns ahead of the upcoming APEC summit. The discussion focused on reviewing economic and trade commitments made over the past year.

UPSC Syllabus Mapping: GS Paper II – Bilateral, regional and global groupings involving India; International Economic Relations; Trade and Investment Policies.

Key Data & Agreements: APEC 2024 (upcoming summit) • Last‑year trade commitments under review

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The dialogue underscores heightened U.S.–China economic rivalry and the role of APEC as a platform where major economies negotiate trade norms, influencing the broader Indo‑Pacific strategic balance.
  • Impact on India: India, as a key APEC participant, may face pressure to align with either bloc on issues such as supply‑chain diversification, technology transfer norms, and market‑access commitments, affecting its own trade agenda and strategic autonomy.
  • Way Forward: India should leverage its non‑aligned stance to advocate for transparent, rules‑based trade, push for inclusive supply‑chain frameworks, and deepen engagement with both the U.S. and China while safeguarding domestic industries.
