Core Summary: Former U.S. National Security Adviser John Bolton criticised Republican candidate Tim Vance for distancing himself from former President Donald Trump, calling it a “big political mistake.” Bolton warned that the GOP is overlooking widespread voter discontent over the economy, rising inflation and Trump‑era tariff policies.

Deep-Dive Analysis

  • Context & Geopolitical Impact: The intra‑party rift in the United States signals potential volatility in U.S. trade and monetary policies, which could reshape the global order, especially in multilateral forums like the WTO and G20.
  • Impact on India: Shifts in U.S. tariff outlook affect India’s export‑import dynamics (e.g., agricultural commodities, pharmaceuticals). A more protectionist U.S. stance could pressure India to diversify markets, while political instability may influence foreign‑direct investment flows.
  • Way Forward: India should deepen engagement with the U.S. through the Quad and Indo‑U.S. Strategic Dialogue, diversify trade partners, and bolster domestic resilience against external price shocks.
